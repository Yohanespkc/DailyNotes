# **Entanglement sebagai *Compressed Shared State***  
*(Formalisme yang rapi dan kompatibel dengan fisika kuantum modern)*

## **1. Intuisi dulu: “state bersama, bukan dua state terpisah”**  
Jika dua qubit A dan B *entangled*, kita **tidak bisa menulis** state total sebagai:

\[
|\psi\rangle_{AB} = |\psi\rangle_A \otimes |\psi\rangle_B.
\]

Artinya:  
- **A dan B tidak punya informasi masing-masing.**  
- Informasi mereka hanya eksis **di struktur gabungannya**.

➡️ Ini persis definisi ***shared state***.

---

# **2. Apa arti “compressed”?**
Misalnya state Bell:

\[
|\Phi^{+}\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|00\rangle + |11\rangle)
\]

Jika kita tulis tabel lengkap untuk dua qubit independen:
- ada **4** kemungkinan: 00, 01, 10, 11

Tetapi entanglement *menghapus* 2 kemungkinan (01, 10)  
dan menyatukan dua sisanya dalam satu struktur.

Informasinya jadi **lebih sedikit**, tetapi **lebih kuat**.

➡️ Inilah yang saya maksud:  
**shared state yang dikompresi oleh korelasi sempurna**.

---

# **3. Definisi matematis (formal minimal)**

## **State terkompresi**
Sebuah state \(|\Psi\rangle_{AB}\) dikatakan **compressed shared** jika:

\[
S(\rho_A) = S(\rho_B) > 0 \quad \text{dan} \quad S(\rho_{AB}) = 0,
\]

dimana \(S(\cdot)\) = entropi von Neumann.

Artinya:
- Seluruh sistem murni (entropi 0)
- Masing-masing subsistem acak (entropi > 0)

Interpretasi:
> **Seluruh informasi tidak ada pada A atau B,  
tetapi hanya ada pada relasinya.**

Itu adalah definisi formal paling bersih dari **entanglement sebagai compressed shared state**.

---

# **4. Kenapa “compressed”? (lebih dalam)**
Untuk dua qubit:

- Sistem penuh butuh **4 amplitudo kompleks**  
  (karena Hilbert space = 2×2 = 4 dimensi)

- Tetapi entangled state **yang sangat terstruktur**  
  seperti Bell pair hanya butuh **1 derajat kebebasan efektif**:
  - apakah mereka sama? atau berbeda?  
  - sign + atau - ?

Lebih detail:
- Kita bisa menyandikan seluruh benda 2 qubit dalam *hanya satu bit logika*:  
  ➤ *“A = B” atau “A ≠ B”*  

Jadi secara kompresi:
- Sebuah struktur 4-dimensi  
- Menjadi 1-dimensi informasi relasi

➡️ **Entanglement = kompresi informasi kuat via korelasi sempurna.**

---

# **5. Kenapa “shared”?**
Karena:

- amplitudo untuk A dan B **tidak terdefinisi sendiri**

\[
|\Phi^{+}\rangle \neq |\psi\rangle_A \otimes |\phi\rangle_B
\]

Tidak mungkin.

Ini bukan sekadar “berhubungan”, tetapi:

> **A dan B tidak memiliki identitas probabilistik sendiri.  
Yang ada hanya identitas relasional.**

Ini definisi *shared state*.

Ini **formal**, bukan kiasan.

## 1. Abstract

This white paper proposes the **GASING-AI Chip**: a **quantum-inspired, classical hardware architecture** designed to perform core arithmetic in (near) constant time using **lookup-table-based ALUs (LUT-ALUs)**, tightly integrated with a **Personal Knowledge Container (PKC)** stack and the **GASING** learning method.

Instead of optimizing for generic floating-point throughput like GPUs, the GASING-AI Chip is specialized for:

- **Fast, ultra-low-energy integer and fixed-point arithmetic**
- **On-device numeracy and STEM learning**
- **Explainable, structure-based computation aligned with human cognition**
- **Edge AI scenarios where power, determinism, and sovereignty matter**

The central thesis is simple:

> **GASING does for human cognition what LUT-ALUs can do for classical hardware:  
> replace iterative procedures with evaluation of compressed structure.**

This document outlines the motivation, conceptual foundation, core architecture, integration with PKC/MCard/PCard/VCard, and potential applications.

## 2. Motivation

### 2.1 Global numeracy crisis

Across the world, students struggle with basic numeracy and mathematical reasoning. Standard digital solutions (tablets, learning apps, generic AI tutors) mostly **deliver content** but do not fundamentally change **how arithmetic is represented and executed**—neither in machines nor in minds.

### 2.2 Hardware mismatch

Modern AI accelerators (GPUs, TPUs) are optimized for:

- High-dimensional **floating-point** linear algebra
- Massive matrix multiplications
- Data center power budgets

But education and embedded learning devices need:

- Deterministic, low-latency **integer and fixed-point arithmetic**
- Extremely low power consumption
- Tight coupling to **human learning models**, not only to statistical models

### 2.3 The opportunity

The **GASING method** shows that with the right **conceptual structure and lookup patterns**, children can perform “hard” arithmetic almost instantaneously. The same principle can be implemented in **classical silicon** using LUT-ALUs:

- **Quantum-inspired:** leverage global structure and constant-time evaluation  
- **Classical:** no qubits, no decoherence, implementable with standard CMOS

The GASING-AI Chip aims to be the **hardware embodiment** of these ideas.

## 3. Conceptual Foundation

### 3.1 GASING as structure-first arithmetic

GASING (Gampang, Asyik, Menyenangkan) teaches arithmetic by:

- Building **strong conceptual structures** (e.g., pairs to 10, anchors, decompositions)
- Using **lookup and pattern recognition**, not long procedures
- Creating **entangled conceptual states**: multiplication, division, and factorization become one compressed structure in the learner’s mind

In essence:

> GASING turns arithmetic from **procedural sequences** into **compressed relational states**.

### 3.2 PKC as “Hilbert space of the human”

PKC (Personal Knowledge Container) models a person’s knowledge as a **state in a high-dimensional space**:

- **MCard**: conceptual basis vectors (core concepts)
- **PCard**: relations / operators between concepts
- **VCard**: proofs / verifiable projections of competence

This mirrors how **Hilbert space** is used in quantum mechanics:  
the system is not defined by positions in physical space, but by a **state vector** in an abstract space of possibilities.

### 3.3 Quantum-inspired classical computation

Quantum computers gain speedup by:

- Representing many possibilities in a single global state
- Letting **structure + interference** guide the outcome
- Avoiding sequential exploration

We can mimic the **structural advantage**—without qubits—by:

- Precomputing operation results into **lookup tables (LUTs)**
- Treating computation as **addressing**, not iterating
- Using **LUT-ALUs** as constant-time evaluators of discrete state

The GASING-AI Chip embraces this **quantum-inspired but classical** philosophy.

## 4. LUT-ALU: Constant-Time Arithmetic Core

### 4.1 Classical ALU vs LUT-ALU

Typical ALUs:

- Perform addition via **ripple-carry** or **carry-lookahead**
- Perform multiplication via **shift-add loops**, partial products
- Perform division via iterative approximations

Latency and energy scale with **bit-width** and **algorithmic complexity**.

In contrast, a **LUT-ALU**:

- Encodes arithmetic relations into **tables**
- Maps inputs `(a, b)` to precomputed results
- Uses one **memory access** as the primary operation

Conceptually:

```text
[a, b] --> encoder --> LUT[address] --> [result, flags]
```

The complexity shifts from **time** to **space** (memory).  
For constrained domains (e.g., 8-bit, 10-bit, 2-digit BCD), this is highly feasible.

### 4.2 Example: 2-digit addition (0–99)

- Input space: `a, b ∈ {0..99}`
- LUT size: 100 × 100 = 10,000 entries
- Per entry: result (0–198), carry, overflow flags

Each addition:

- Latency ≈ 1 ± ε clock (memory read)
- No carry-propagation steps
- Energy consumed dominated by a single memory access

This closely matches how GASING “jumps” to answers via structured recall.

### 4.3 Beyond brute-force LUT

To keep area and memory efficient, the LUT-ALU can be **factored**:

- Per-digit LUTs (e.g., base-10 or base-16)
- Hierarchical structure (e.g., units, tens, hundreds)
- Separate LUTs for special micro-patterns (e.g., “pair to 10”)

This mirrors GASING’s pedagogy:

- Start from strong core structures (pairs to 10, base decompositions)
- Compose them to handle larger numbers

This block:

- Maintains a **local PKC state** for each learner or task
- Uses **MCard** to represent mastered conceptual anchors (e.g., “pairs to 10”, “decomposition into tens and ones”)
- Uses **PCard** to track **relationships** and **operator applications** (e.g., child can combine Add10 + decomposition)
- Emits **VCard** as compressed, verifiable evidence:
  - “This device/learner can reliably solve 2-digit addition in 1 step”
  - Without transmitting raw interaction logs

### 5.3 Operator Engine (GASING micro-operations)

This layer implements **GASING operators** such as:

- `U_Add10` (pairing to 10 and propagating to higher place values)
- `U_Balance` (algebraic balance operator for equations like `x + a = b`)
- `U_Anchor` (using anchors like 25, 50, 100 for mental arithmetic)
- `U_Decompose` (splitting numbers into structured parts)

These are encoded as:

- **Microcode sequences** driving LUT-ALU calls
- Or small deterministic state machines
- Aligning hardware execution with GASING pedagogy

### 5.4 LUT Arithmetic Core

Contains specialized LUTs for:

- Integer **addition/subtraction** (bounded ranges, e.g. 2-digit, 3-digit)
- Integer **multiplication/division** within well-defined domains
- **GASING-specific patterns**:
  - `a + b` where `(a, b)` are near anchors (10, 100, etc.)
  - factor pairs, small primes, etc.

The core is:

- Highly **deterministic**
- Low-latency and **low-jitter**
- Power-thrifty compared to general-purpose ALUs for the same domain

U_{\text{Add10}}(a,b) =

- Decompose: 40+7, 30+8  
- Satuan: 7+8 → via `U_Add10`  
  - 7+3=10, sisa 5 → 15 (satuan 5, carry 1)  
- Puluhan: 4+3+1 = 8  
- Hasil: 85

**Implementasi LUT:**  
- LUT satuan 10×10  
- LUT puluhan 10×10 (dengan carry)

## 3. Operator Pengurangan ( − )

### 3.1. `U_SubComplement10` – kurang lewat komplemen 10

**Fungsi:**  
Gunakan konsep “pinjam 10” dari puluhan, tapi dipahami sebagai pasangan 10.

**Formal kasar (1 digit):**
\[
U_{\text{Sub10}}(a,b) =
\begin{cases}
(a-b, 0) & a \ge b\\
(10 + a - b, -1) & a < b \ \text{(pinjam 1 dari puluhan)}
\end{cases}
\]

**Contoh:**  
95 − 29 (versi GASING yang kamu tulis)
- Puluhan: 9 − 2 → 7, tapi nanti disesuaikan karena pinjam
- Satuan: 5 − 9 →  
  - pakai komplemen 10: 5 + (10−9) = 6  
  - pinjam 1 → puluhan 7 jadi 6  
- Hasil: 66

**Inti operator:**  
- `U_Pair10(9) = 1` → 10−9  
- Satuan jadi 5 + 1 = 6  
- Puluhan dikurangi 1

**Implementasi LUT:**  
LUT `(a_s, b_s)` berisi `(hasilSatuan, deltaPuluhan)`;  
puluhan disesuaikan kemudian.

---

### 3.2. `U_SubDecompose` – kurang multi-digit

Mirip `AddDecompose`, tapi pakai `SubComplement10` di satuan:

1. `(a_t, a_s) = U_Decompose(a, 10)`  
2. `(b_t, b_s) = U_Decompose(b, 10)`  
3. `U_Sub10(a_s, b_s) → (satuanBaru, deltaPuluhan)`  
4. `puluhanBaru = a_t - b_t + deltaPuluhan`

## 4. Operator Perkalian ( × )

### 4.1. `U_MulGrid` – model kotak (array)

**Fungsi:**  
Memecah n×m menjadi luasan kotak-kotak (bagi anak visual).

**Formal:**
- Decompose: `a = a_t·10 + a_s`, `b = b_t·10 + b_s`  
- Hitung blok:
  - `a_t·b_t·100`
  - `a_t·b_s·10`
  - `a_s·b_t·10`
  - `a_s·b_s`
- Gabungkan dengan `AddDecompose`.

**Contoh:**  
23 × 14  
- 20×10 = 200  
- 20×4 = 80  
- 3×10 = 30  
- 3×4 = 12  
- Total = 322

**Implementasi LUT:**  
- LUT 10×10 untuk tabel perkalian satu digit  
- Operator `Decompose` + beberapa `Add`.

---

### 4.2. `U_MulAnchor` – kali dekat jangkar (10, 100, …)

**Fungsi:**  
Khusus trik seperti **99×37**, **101×48**, dll.

**Formal (contoh 100):**
1. `U_Anchor(a, 100) → (100, Δa)`  
2. `U_Anchor(b, 100) → (100, Δb)`  
3. `(100 + Δa)(100 + Δb) = 10000 + 100(Δa+Δb) + ΔaΔb`  
   → pakai LUT kecil bilangan Δ (kecil).

**GASING style 99×37:**
- 99 = 100−1
- (100−1)×37 = 3700−37 = 3663

**Operator ringkas:**
\[
U_{\text{MulAnchor}}(a,b,A)
\]

---

### 4.3. `U_MulRepeatedAddStruct` – versi konseptual

Bukan repeated addition bodoh, tapi:
- dekorasi ke `U_Decompose` dan `U_MulGrid`  
- cocok untuk implementasi hardware modul kecil.

## 5. Operator Pembagian ( ÷ )

Inti GASING: **pembagian = “balik” dari perkalian** dengan struktur.

### 5.1. `U_DivFactorPair` – cari pasangan faktor

**Fungsi:**  
Untuk kasus kecil: cari `p × q = N`.

**Formal:**
\[
U_{\text{DivFactorPair}}(N, \text{range}) \rightarrow (p,q)
\quad\text{dengan}\quad p\cdot q = N
\]

Di otak anak:
- banyak `p×q` sudah di-entangle lewat tabel perkalian
- jadi ini **lookup**, bukan trial-and-error berat.

**Implementasi LUT:**  
- LUT mapping dari `N` → `(p,q)` untuk N kecil (misal ≤ 100).

---

### 5.2. `U_DivChunkAnchor` – bagi dengan jangkar

Contoh: 432 ÷ 8  
- 8 dekat 10, atau 8=2×4, dll.  
Satu versi:
1. `432 ÷ 8 = (400 + 32) ÷ 8`  
2. `400 ÷ 8 = 50`, `32 ÷ 8 = 4`  
3. Hasil 54

**Formal:**
- `U_Decompose(N, 100/10)`  
- `U_DivSmall` pada tiap bagian  
- gabungkan dengan `Add`.

**Implementasi LUT:**  
- LUT untuk `k÷d` di range kecil (`k<100`, `d<10`).

## 6. Operator Akar ( √ )

### 6.1. `U_SqrtArea` – akar sebagai sisi persegi

**Fungsi:**  
Untuk bilangan kuadrat sempurna kecil (1–225, dll.)

**Formal:**
\[
U_{\text{SqrtArea}}(N) \rightarrow s \quad \text{dengan} \quad s^2 = N
\]

Di PKC:
- `N` dan `s` di-entangle dalam satu struktur (seperti 36 ↔ 6).

Anak:
- Mengenal area 1×1, 2×2, …, 15×15  
- Disimpan di “tabel akar” mental GASING.

**Implementasi LUT:**  
- LUT kecil: `N → s` untuk kuadrat sempurna  
- `N → (floor_s, ceil_s)` untuk lainnya.

---

### 6.2. `U_SqrtDecompose` – dekat kuadrat terdekat

Untuk kasar: √N dengan N bukan kuadrat sempurna.

1. Cari anchor kuadrat terdekat: `A = s²`.
2. Hitung selisih `Δ = N − A`.
3. Perbaiki estimasi menggunakan pola (linear approx atau lookup kecil).

Ini bisa jadi tingkat lanjut, tapi konsepnya:
> akar = “sedikit di atas” atau “di bawah” sisi persegi terdekat.

## 7. Operator Aljabar

Ini yang kamu sebut tadi: `U_Balance`, dll.

### 7.1. `U_Balance` – persamaan sebagai timbangan

**Fungsi:**  
Menyelesaikan persamaan linear satu langkah: `x + a = b`, `x − a = b`.

**Formal:**
\[
U_{\text{Balance}}(x + a = b) \rightarrow x = b - a
\]
\[
U_{\text{Balance}}(x - a = b) \rightarrow x = b + a
\]

Tapi di GASING:
- anak **tidak** diajar “pindah ruas jadi minus”
- anak **melakukan operasi yang sama di kedua sisi timbangan**

**Implementasi:**  
- Representasi internal: `(LHS,RHS)`  
- Operator: `LHS − a`, `RHS − a` secara serempak.

---

### 7.2. `U_Balance2` – 2x + a = b

Untuk bentuk `k·x + a = b` (k kecil):

1. `U_Balance` → `k·x = b − a`  
2. `U_DivSmall` (pembagian kecil): `x = (b − a)/k`

**Contoh:**  
2x + 3 = 11  
- Balance: 2x = 8  
- Div: x = 4

---

### 7.3. `U_Substitute` – substitusi sederhana

Dipakai saat:
- `y = 2x + 3`  
- lalu ada `x = 5`

Operator:  
\[
U_{\text{Substitute}}(y=f(x), x=x_0) \rightarrow y=f(x_0)
\]

Ini penting untuk menjembatani aritmatika ↔ aljabar.

- **MCard inti:** M-PAIR10, M-ANCHOR-10, M-ADD-BASIC, M-ADD-CARRY, M-DECOMP  
- **PCard tujuan:** **P-ADD10-PAIR**, **P-ADD10-CARRY**, **P-PAIR10-SET**, **P-ADD-DEC**

---

## **Level Map (10 Level Awal)**

### **Level 1 — Mengenal Pasangan 10**
**Gameplay:**  
- Di layar ada angka 1–9.  
- Tugas: pasangkan angka yang **jumlahnya 10**.

**Contoh puzzle:**  
- Seret 7 → sistem menyorot 3.

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** M-PAIR10  
- **PCard:** *(belum)* — pengenalan basis

✅ **Lulus jika:** anak cepat menemukan pasangan tanpa mencoba acak.

---

### **Level 2 — Pasangan 10 Acak**
**Gameplay:**  
- Banyak angka muncul bersamaan.  
- Waktu dibatasi ringan.

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** M-PAIR10  
- **PCard:** **P-PAIR10-SET**

✅ **Lulus jika:** pasangan 10 muncul **refleks** (tanpa jeda).

---

### **Level 3 — Pasangan 10 + Visual Band**
**Gameplay:**  
- Pasangan 10 membentuk **blok penuh** (visual bar terisi).  

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** M-ANCHOR-10  
- **PCard:** **P-PAIR10-SET**

✅ **Makna:** “10 itu satu unit utuh.”

---

### **Level 4 — Dari Pasangan ke Penjumlahan (≤10)**
**Gameplay:**  
- Soal implisit: gabungkan dua angka untuk mengisi bar 10.

**Contoh:**  
- 6 + 4 → bar penuh

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** M-ADD-BASIC  
- **PCard:** **P-ADD10-PAIR**

✅ **Makna:** penjumlahan = cari pasangan 10 (bukan hitung).

---

### **Level 5 — Melewati 10 (Carry Pertama)**
**Gameplay:**  
- Jika >10, bar 10 “berangkat”, sisa tetap.

**Contoh:**  
- 7 + 5 → satu bar 10 + sisa 2

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** M-ADD-CARRY  
- **PCard:** **P-ADD10-CARRY**

✅ **Makna:** “bawa” lahir alami dari pasangan 10.

---

### **Level 6 — Otomatisasi Add10**
**Gameplay:**  
- Puzzle cepat beruntun (7+8, 6+9, 8+7).

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** (penguatan)  
- **PCard:** **P-ADD10-AUTO** *(internal threshold)*

✅ **Lulus jika:** waktu respon konsisten cepat → PCard stabil.

---

### **Level 7 — Masuk Dekomposisi (2 Digit Ringan)**
**Gameplay:**  
- 14 + 6, 18 + 7 (disajikan sebagai bar + bar kecil).

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** M-DECOMP  
- **PCard:** **P-ADD-DEC (awal)**

✅ **Makna:** satuan dipisah dari “puluhan mini”.

---

### **Level 8 — Dua Digit Sungguhan**
**Gameplay:**  
- 27 + 5, 36 + 8.  
- Game otomatis memecah puluhan & satuan.

**Target PKC:**  
- **MCard:** M-DECOMP, M-ADD-CARRY  
- **PCard:** **P-ADD-DEC**, **P-ADD10-CARRY**

✅ **Makna:** Add10 bekerja lintas digit.

---

### **Level 9 — Tantangan Cepat & Campur**
**Gameplay:**  
- Campuran 1-digit & 2-digit, tanpa petunjuk visual berlebihan.

**Target PKC:**  
- **PCard:** **P-ADD10-PAIR, P-ADD-DEC (konsolidasi)**

✅ **Makna:** struktur sudah menyatu (entangled).

---

### **Level 10 — Mini-Boss: “Penjaga Puluhan”**
**Gameplay:**  
- Serangkaian soal berantai (mis. 28+7 → hasil langsung dipakai ke soal berikutnya).  

**Target PKC:**  
- **PCard:** *stabil & konsisten* untuk semua Add10 terkait.

🎉 **Reward:**  
- **VCard:** *“Add10 Mastery”*  
- Hak membuka **Dunia Pengurangan (Sub10)** atau **Dunia Aljabar (Balance awal)**.

---

## **Aturan Kelulusan PKC (di balik layar)**
- **MCard aktif:** ≥ ambang frekuensi benar  
- **PCard stabil:** ≥ ambang kecepatan + konsistensi  
- **VCard terbit:** jika **P-ADD10-PAIR** dan **P-ADD10-CARRY** stabil

> Tidak ada skor akhir palsu—yang ada **pergeseran state PKC**.
