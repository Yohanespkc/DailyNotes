**Economic Census** is a comprehensive survey conducted by a government or statistical agency to gather detailed information about the economic activities of businesses and industries within a country or specific region. The goal of an economic census is to provide accurate data on the structure, performance, and contribution of various sectors to the economy.

This census typically collects data on aspects such as:

1. **Number of establishments** - Counting businesses and industries.
2. **Employment** - Total number of employees in these establishments.
3. **Production and Sales** - Revenue, production levels, and sales figures.
4. **Investment** - Capital investments in equipment, buildings, and other assets.
5. **Business Size** - Categorizing businesses by size based on employees or revenue.
6. **Sectoral Performance** - Insights into specific industries such as manufacturing, trade, services, etc.

The data from an economic census is crucial for governments, policymakers, and businesses as it helps in:

- **Economic Planning:** Guiding policies to improve economic growth and development.
- **Business Decisions:** Providing insights for private sector investment and expansion.
- **Employment Strategies:** Identifying opportunities to generate more jobs.
- **Resource Allocation:** Helping allocate resources effectively to promote balanced growth.

Economic censuses are typically conducted at regular intervals (e.g., every 5 or 10 years) to ensure updated and relevant data for decision-making.
