The terms "besaran pokok" and "besaran turunan" in English are translated as:

- **Besaran Pokok**: Fundamental Quantities or Base Quantities
- **Besaran Turunan**: Derived Quantities

These terms are used in physics and other sciences to distinguish between quantities that are fundamental and directly measurable (such as time, length, and mass) and those that are derived from the fundamental quantities (such as speed, force, and energy).

Dalam konteks fisika, berikut adalah terjemahan yang lebih tepat:

- **Besaran Pokok**: **Fundamental Quantities** atau **Base Quantities**
