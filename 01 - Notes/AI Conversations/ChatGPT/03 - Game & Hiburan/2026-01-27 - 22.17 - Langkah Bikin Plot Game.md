## 4) Buat “Flowchart (bagan alur)” minimal: **Main Path + Fail Loop**
Untuk game investigasi, cukup 2 jalur:

- **Main Path (alur utama):** pemain menemukan clue → solve puzzle → naik babak.  
- **Fail Loop (putaran gagal):** salah jawab → dapat feedback → balik ke hotspot yang “belum kebaca”.

Contoh: Stage Pintu  
- Kalau pilih H1/H2, feedback mengarah balik ke observasi mekanisme & celah. fileciteturn1file1L21-L30  
- Baru H3 membuka deduksi final. fileciteturn1file1L33-L44

# Paket Prompt Nano Banana (siap pakai)
Saya buatkan **judul Indonesia**, dan istilah asing saya beri terjemahan Indonesia dalam tanda kurung.

## A) “Storyboard Kasus 2” (8 frame)
**Judul:** *Storyboard (papan cerita) — THE QUIET ROOM (Kasus 2)*  
**Prompt (English untuk Nano Banana):**  
“Create an 8-panel storyboard, cinematic detective thriller, consistent characters across panels. Panel 1: parents panicking outside a locked bedroom door at dawn. Panel 2: detective team (Tofi, Rahul, William) enters hallway, serious mood. Panel 3: close-up of old rotating door lever with fresh scrape marks. Panel 4: close-up latch mechanism slightly loose. Panel 5: thin tool inserted through the side gap of the door, demonstrating shim bypass. Panel 6: desk with an overly perfect ‘suicide note’ next to a diary. Panel 7: fallen digital clock on the floor near the body. Panel 8: window frame carving with symbols ‘π • φ • G’ and the word ‘log’, ominous. Visual style: realistic, dramatic lighting, clean readable props, no gore.”

## B) “Layout Kamar Clara” (tampak atas)
**Judul:** *Denah (layout) Kamar Clara — Bukti TKP*  
**Prompt:**  
“Top-down room layout blueprint of Clara’s bedroom, clean and readable. Show bed, desk, door with side gap, window, location of fallen digital clock on the floor, body position marker (non-graphic silhouette outline), and droplet pattern area near the door. Add labeled callouts with numbers 1–8. Style: forensic diagram, high clarity, white background, thin lines.”

## C) “Close-up Pintu: 3 Hotspot”
**Judul:** *Zoom Pintu — Tuas, Latch, Celah*  
**Prompt:**  
“Create a UI-style zoom-in image of a bedroom door for an investigation game. Show three clickable hotspots with subtle glowing markers: (1) old rotating metal lever with wear and fresh scrape marks, (2) latch mechanism slightly loose, (3) side gap about 1.8 cm. Style: realistic game UI, sharp details, no text clutter, suspense mood.”

## D) “Puzzle Kode Jendela”
**Judul:** *Kode Bingkai Jendela — π • φ • G + log*  
**Prompt:**  
“Cinematic close-up of a window frame with a neat carved code: ‘π • φ • G’ and below it the small word ‘log’. Add soft desk lamp glow, quiet eerie atmosphere, detective thriller style. Keep the symbols crisp and legible.”

## 6) Checklist produksi (biar plot Anda “jadi game”)
Urutan kerja yang paling efisien:

1. **Beat Sheet 5 babak** (sudah ada; rapikan 1 halaman). fileciteturn1file0L17-L71  
2. Buat **10–15 Scene Card** (tiap stage 1 kartu).  
3. Dari tiap Scene Card, buat **Asset List (daftar aset)**:  
   - Background (kamar/koridor)  
   - Props (surat, buku harian, jam digital, bingkai jendela)  
   - UI overlays (marker hotspot, grid analisis, timeline)  
4. Baru generate di Nano Banana:  
   - **Storyboard 8 frame** dulu (untuk memastikan pacing/ketegangan).  
   - Lanjut **layout kamar** + **close-up clue** (untuk puzzle clarity).
