### 🤖 ChatGPT:
The **Five Stages of Grief** is a model proposed by psychiatrist **Elisabeth Kübler-Ross** to describe common emotional responses people may experience after loss. They are:

1. **Denial** — Shock or disbelief. Thoughts like *“This can’t be happening.”*
2. **Anger** — Frustration or resentment. *“Why is this happening to me?”*
3. **Bargaining** — Attempting to negotiate or make deals (often with fate, God, or oneself). *“If only I had…”*
4. **Depression** — Deep sadness, withdrawal, or hopelessness as reality sinks in.
5. **Acceptance** — Coming to terms with the loss and beginning to move forward.

**Important:**  
These stages are **not linear** and not everyone experiences all of them. People may move back and forth between stages, skip some, or experience them in different orders.

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Di **Microsoft Word**, agar gambar yang dipaste otomatis menjadi **full size (maksimal lebar halaman)**, ada beberapa cara — pilih yang paling praktis:

---

## ✅ Cara 1 — Atur Default Layout Gambar (sekali set, berlaku terus)
1. **File → Options**
2. Pilih **Advanced**
3. Scroll ke bagian **Cut, copy, and paste**
4. Pada opsi **Insert/paste pictures as**, pilih  
   👉 **In line with text** (atau *Square* kalau ingin bebas geser)
5. Klik **OK**

➡️ Ini memastikan gambar tidak kecil otomatis saat dipaste.

---

## ✅ Cara 2 — Resize Otomatis Full Lebar Halaman (manual cepat)
Setelah paste gambar:

1. Klik gambar
2. Tekan **Ctrl + A** (pilih gambar)
3. Masuk tab **Picture Format**
4. Di bagian **Size**, ubah **Width** menjadi:
   - ± **16 cm** (untuk A4 margin normal)
   - ± **14.8 cm** (margin besar)
5. Tekan Enter

---

## ✅ Cara 3 — Supaya Sekali Paste Langsung Full Lebar (Trik Cepat)
1. Paste gambar
2. Klik kanan gambar → **Size and Position**
3. Set:
   - Width → misalnya **16 cm**
   - Centang **Lock aspect ratio**
4. Klik **Set As Default**

➡️ Setelah itu semua gambar yang dimasukkan akan otomatis mengikuti ukuran ini.

---

## 🖨️ Cara Stop Printing di Komputer
