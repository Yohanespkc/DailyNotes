# 💬 Enhancement Prompt Buku

**Tanggal:** `2026-01-12 20.06`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
buat prompt untuk membuat gambar disebelah kiri lebih jernih dan siap dipublikasi dalam buku. QR Code tidak boleh berubah dan harus bisa discan

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Siap. Berikut **prompt yang bisa langsung kamu pakai** (untuk Photoshop Generative Fill, Topaz, Magnific, Leonardo, Midjourney image-edit, atau AI enhancement lain):

---

### 🎯 **PROMPT ENHANCEMENT GAMBAR (SIAP CETAK BUKU)**

> **Task:** Professional photo enhancement for book publication.

Enhance the **left photo only** to become **sharp, clear, and print-ready**, while **preserving the original composition**.

**Requirements (VERY IMPORTANT):**
- ❗ **DO NOT modify, regenerate, stylize, crop, distort, or redraw the QR Code on the right**
- ❗ **QR Code must remain 100% identical, untouched, and fully scannable**
- ❗ **No changes in QR Code size, contrast, alignment, or pixel structure**
- ❗ **Only enhance the left image**

**Enhancement goals for the left image:**
- Remove blur and motion softness → **high clarity, crisp focus**
- Improve lighting balance (natural indoor light, no overexposure)
- Correct white balance (neutral whites, accurate skin tones)
- Reduce noise and compression artifacts
- Increase micro-detail on faces, clothing, and background objects
- Maintain realistic textures (no plastic skin, no painterly effect)
- Preserve original framing and proportions (no cropping)
- Subtle contrast and color grading suitable for **academic / documentary book**
- Clean, professional, editorial photography look

**Style reference:**
- Documentary education photography
- Natural, respectful, formal
- Suitable for printed books (300 dpi feeling)
- No cinematic filters, no dramatic effects, no stylization

**Output specifications:**
- Aspect ratio unchanged
- Print-ready quality
- Looks like a high-end DSLR photo taken in good lighting

---

### 🛑 **NEGATIVE PROMPT (PENTING):**
> blurry, over-sharpened halos, HDR look, AI artifacts, face distortion, cartoon style, painting style, stylized lighting, QR code alteration, QR blur, QR regeneration, cropping, perspective change

---

- ✨ Buat versi **khusus Magnific / Topaz / Photoshop**
- 📐 Sesuaikan **standar cetak buku (A4 / A5 / 300 dpi)**
- 📘 Samakan gaya visual dengan halaman buku GASING lainnya

Tinggal bilang mau dipakai di **tool apa** 👍

### 👤 User:
buat prompt yang memperjelas suatu gambar sehingga siap publish dalam buku. Gambar tidak akan pecah ketika diprint. warna yang blur menjadi lebih terang. 

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Siap. Berikut **prompt generik tapi kuat** yang bisa kamu pakai di **hampir semua AI image enhancement / upscaling tool**, khusus untuk **siap cetak buku**:

---

## 🎯 PROMPT ENHANCEMENT GAMBAR (SIAP CETAK BUKU)

> **Task:** Professional image enhancement and print-ready upscaling.

Enhance this image to become **sharp, clear, and high-resolution**, suitable for **book publication and high-quality printing**.

**Enhancement objectives:**
- Remove blur and softness → **crisp, well-defined details**
- Increase clarity without over-sharpening
- Restore fine textures (faces, clothing, objects, text)
- Improve color brightness for previously blurred or dull areas
- Correct white balance so colors look natural and clean
- Increase contrast subtly to improve readability in print
- Reduce noise and compression artifacts
- Maintain realistic colors (no oversaturation)
- Preserve original composition and proportions (no cropping)

**Print-ready requirements:**
- Image must **not break, pixelate, or blur when printed**
- Output should feel like **300 DPI print quality**
- Suitable for academic / documentary / educational book
- Natural lighting, professional editorial look

**Style:**
- Clean, realistic, documentary photography
- No artistic stylization
- No cinematic or HDR effects
- No artificial filters

---

## 🚫 NEGATIVE PROMPT (PENTING)

> over-sharpening, halo edges, plastic skin, cartoon, painting, illustration, HDR, color banding, artifacts, distortion, crop, perspective change, fake lighting

---

### 💡 Tips Pemakaian
- Jika tool punya opsi **“enhance only / upscale + enhance” → pilih itu**
- Jika ada pilihan **2× atau 4× upscale → pilih 2× dulu untuk buku**
- Hindari “creative” atau “stylize” mode

Kalau mau, aku bisa:
- Menyesuaikan prompt ini untuk **Midjourney / Leonardo / Photoshop / Topaz / Magnific**
- Buat versi **khusus foto manusia, foto dokumen, atau ilustrasi buku**
- Sesuaikan dengan **ukuran cetak (A4 / A5 / A6)**

Tinggal bilang 👍
