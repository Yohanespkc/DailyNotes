## 🎯 PROMPT EDIT GAMBAR (HIGH-QUALITY BOOK READY)

> **Task:** Image enhancement & layout refinement for book publishing  
>  
> **Instructions:**  
> Tingkatkan kualitas gambar ini dengan **super-resolution** agar **tajam, detail, dan tidak blur**, sambil **mempertahankan isi visual asli**.  
>  
> **WAJIB & KRITIS:**  
> - **QR Code TIDAK BOLEH DIUBAH SAMA SEKALI**  
>   - Tidak di-crop  
>   - Tidak di-resize  
>   - Tidak di-recolor  
>   - Tidak di-retouch  
>   - Tidak di-sharpen secara selektif  
>   - Pola piksel QR Code harus **identik 100%** agar tetap dapat dipindai  
>  
> **Pengolahan gambar utama (selain QR Code):**  
> - Hilangkan blur menggunakan **AI super-resolution & deblurring**  
> - Tingkatkan ketajaman wajah, ekspresi, dan gerak tubuh anak-anak  
> - Perbaiki pencahayaan agar natural (tidak overexposed, tidak terlalu kontras)  
> - Warna kulit alami, tidak plastik, tidak HDR berlebihan  
> - Detail pakaian, tangan, dan ekspresi sosial harus jelas  
>  
> **Komposisi & Layout:**  
> - **Background putih / transparan di luar gambar utama dibuang sepenuhnya**  
> - Rapikan tepi gambar dengan **crop presisi dan simetris**  
> - Bentuk akhir **bersih, rapi, dan proporsional** untuk buku (rasio ±3:2 atau A5-ready)  
> - Fokus visual pada interaksi anak-anak (nilai kolaborasi & keceriaan)  
> - QR Code tetap berada di posisi aslinya dan utuh  
>  
> **Gaya akhir:**  
> - Dokumenter edukasi  
> - Natural, hangat, humanis  
> - Siap cetak buku resolusi tinggi (300 dpi look)  
>  
> **Larangan keras:**  
> - Jangan mengganti wajah, ekspresi, atau posisi subjek  
> - Jangan menambah elemen visual baru  
> - Jangan mengubah logo atau teks apa pun  
> - Jangan melakukan artistic stylization  
>  
> **Output:**  
> Gambar tajam, bersih, profesional, **book-publishing quality**, dengan QR Code tetap **100% valid & scannable**.

### 🔒 CRITICAL IMMUTABLE RULE (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
**QR CODE MUST REMAIN 100% UNCHANGED.**

- Do **NOT** crop the QR Code  
- Do **NOT** resize the QR Code  
- Do **NOT** recolor the QR Code  
- Do **NOT** retouch the QR Code  
- Do **NOT** sharpen the QR Code  
- Do **NOT** apply filters to the QR Code  
- Pixel pattern of the QR Code must be **bit-for-bit identical**  
- QR Code must remain **fully scannable and intact**

⚠️ **Any modification to the QR Code is strictly forbidden.**

---

### 🖼️ IMAGE PROCESSING (EXCLUDING QR CODE ONLY)

- Remove blur using **AI super-resolution and deblurring**
- Increase clarity of faces, expressions, and body gestures
- Improve lighting to be **natural and balanced**
  - No overexposure  
  - No harsh contrast  
- Skin tones must look **realistic and human**
  - No plastic look  
  - No excessive HDR  
- Clothing textures, hands, and social interaction details must be crisp

---

### 📐 COMPOSITION & LAYOUT
- Remove **background outside the main photo area** (white / empty margins)
- Crop the **photo area only** with **precise, symmetrical edges**
- Final composition must be:
  - Clean  
  - Balanced  
  - Book-ready  
- Aspect ratio suitable for print (≈ **3:2** or **A5-ready**)
- Visual focus on **human interaction, collaboration, and joy**
- **QR Code stays in its original position and scale**

---

### 🎨 STYLE & TONE
- Educational documentary photography
- Natural, warm, humanistic
- Realistic colors
- No stylization, no artistic filters

---

### 🚫 STRICT PROHIBITIONS
- Do NOT change faces, expressions, or poses
- Do NOT add or remove people or objects
- Do NOT modify logos or text
- Do NOT apply illustration, painting, or cinematic styles
- Do NOT alter perspective or geometry

Produce a **sharp, clean, professional, book-publishing-quality image**  
with **300-dpi print-ready appearance**,
