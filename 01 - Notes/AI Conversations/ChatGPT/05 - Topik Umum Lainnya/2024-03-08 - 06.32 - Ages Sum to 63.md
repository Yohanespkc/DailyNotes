### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Let's break down the problem:

Let:
- Your current age be x
- My current age be y

According to the given information:

1. "I have the age you had when I had the age you have."
   This means when I was x years old, you were y years old.

2. "When you'll have the age I have, together we will have 63 years."
   This implies that when you are y + (y - x) years old, our combined ages will be 63 years.

So, let's solve this:

1. When you are y + (y - x) years old, I'll be x + (y - x) = y years old.
2. At that time, our combined age will be 63: y + (y + (y - x)) = 63.

Combining the equations:

2y + (y - x) = 63
3y - x = 63   ...(1)

From the first given statement, we also know that when I was x years old, you were y years old.

So, x = y - (y - x), which simplifies to x = 2y - x. 

Substitute this into equation (1):

3y - (2y - x) = 63
3y - 2y + x = 63
y + x = 63

Now, we have two equations:

1. y + x = 63
2. 3y - x = 63

From equation (1), we can find x in terms of y:

x = 63 - y

Substitute this into equation (2):

3y - (63 - y) = 63
3y - 63 + y = 63
4y - 63 = 63
4y = 126
y = 31.5

Now, we can find x:

x = 63 - y
x = 63 - 31.5
x = 31.5

However, ages are typically integers, so we can't have half years. This suggests there might be an error or ambiguity in the problem statement. If we consider 31.5 as the average of 31 and 32, it would mean you and I are both currently 31 years old. Then, when you turn 32, I would turn 32 as well, and at that point, our combined age would be 63. Therefore, individually, we are both currently 31 years old.
