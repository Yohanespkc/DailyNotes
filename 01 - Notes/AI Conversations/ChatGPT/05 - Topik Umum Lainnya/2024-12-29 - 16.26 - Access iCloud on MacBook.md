# 💬 Access iCloud on MacBook

**Tanggal:** `2024-12-29 16.26`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
Please tell me how to access files I already stored on iCloud to my new MacBook

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
To access files stored on iCloud on your new MacBook, follow these steps:

### **1. Sign in to iCloud on your MacBook**
   - Go to **System Settings** (or **System Preferences**, depending on your macOS version).
   - Click on your **Apple ID** at the top (if you're not already signed in).
   - Sign in with your **Apple ID** and password.
   - Ensure iCloud is enabled.

### **2. Enable iCloud Drive**
   - While in the **Apple ID** settings, look for **iCloud** in the sidebar.
   - Ensure **iCloud Drive** is toggled ON.
   - Click **Options** next to iCloud Drive and ensure the folders or apps you want (like Desktop & Documents) are selected.

### **3. Access iCloud Files in Finder**
   - Open the **Finder** app (the smiling face icon on your Dock).
   - In the Finder sidebar, look for **iCloud Drive** under the **iCloud** section.
   - Click on **iCloud Drive** to see and access all your files stored there.

### **4. Download Files from iCloud Drive**
   - Files stored in iCloud may appear with a cloud icon next to them.
   - To download a file to your MacBook, simply double-click it, or right-click and select **Download Now**.

### **5. Access iCloud via Browser (Optional)**
   - If you want to check or download files without syncing everything, go to [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) in a web browser.
   - Log in with your Apple ID and access **iCloud Drive** to view and download files manually.

### **6. Sync Specific Apps (Optional)**
   - If you want apps like Photos, Notes, or Calendar to sync, go back to the **iCloud** settings and enable the toggles for each app you wish to sync.

Once these steps are completed, your iCloud files and data should be fully accessible on your new MacBook!
