# 💬 Rounding Rule: Round to Even

**Tanggal:** `2024-03-25 22.46`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
what is the rule for rounding 5

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
The rule for rounding numbers ending in 5 typically follows the convention of rounding to the nearest even number, also known as "banker's rounding" or "round to even." 

For example:
- If the digit preceding the 5 is even, the 5 is rounded down.
- If the digit preceding the 5 is odd, the 5 is rounded up.

This method helps to mitigate rounding bias that might occur when rounding numbers in a series of calculations. It aims to evenly distribute rounding errors, statistically reducing the impact of rounding towards either higher or lower values.
