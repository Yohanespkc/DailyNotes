### **Early Healing (First 2–3 Weeks)**  
- Swelling and bruising around the nose and eyes are most noticeable in the first week.  
- Cast/splints are often removed around day 7–10.

### **Intermediate Stage (1–3 Months)**  
- Major swelling goes down significantly.  
- Breathing improves if functional corrections were made.  
- The nose begins to show a more refined contour, but tip and bridge may still look “puffy.”  

### **Long-Term Results (6–12 Months)**  
- The nose continues to settle and refine.  
- Rib cartilage grafts can take longer to integrate, so final shape and definition are often visible closer to **12 months**.  
- Subtle improvements may continue up to **18–24 months** after surgery, especially for thick skin or major reconstructive cases.

- The rib incision (on the chest) can feel sore for several weeks — so jobs involving upper body effort may need more healing time.
