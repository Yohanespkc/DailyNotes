what is x if a = 2 for x=a/\pi - ( sqrt(a)/a)  *( sqrt(a+sqrt(a))/a)*( sqrt(a+sqrt(a+sqrt(a)))/a )

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
The given equation is:

\[
x = \frac{a}{\pi} - \left( \frac{\sqrt{a}}{a} \right) \cdot \left( \frac{\sqrt{a + \sqrt{a}}}{a} \right) \cdot \left( \frac{\sqrt{a + \sqrt{a + \sqrt{a}}}}{a} \right)
\]

### Step 1: Substitute \(a = 2\)
\[
x = \frac{2}{\pi} - \left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right) \cdot \left( \frac{\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}{2} \right) \cdot \left( \frac{\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}}{2} \right)
\]

### Step 2: Simplify each term
#### First term:
\[
\frac{2}{\pi}
\]

#### Second term:
\[
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}{2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}} \cdot \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}}{8}
\]

Thus, the expression becomes:
\[
x = \frac{2}{\pi} - \frac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}} \cdot \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}}{8}
\]

### Step 3: Numerical evaluation
#### Approximate values:
- \(\pi \approx 3.14159\)
- \(\sqrt{2} \approx 1.41421\)
- \(\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}} \approx \sqrt{2 + 1.41421} \approx \sqrt{3.41421} \approx 1.84776\)
- \(\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}} \approx \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + 1.41421}} \approx \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{3.41421}} \approx \sqrt{2 + 1.84776} \approx \sqrt{3.84776} \approx 1.96157\)

Substitute these values:
- First term: \(\frac{2}{\pi} \approx \frac{2}{3.14159} \approx 0.63662\)
- Second term:
  \[
  \frac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}} \cdot \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}}}{8} \approx \frac{1.41421 \cdot 1.84776 \cdot 1.96157}{8} \approx \frac{5.12796}{8} \approx 0.64099
  \]

### Step 4: Final value
\[
x \approx 0.63662 - 0.64099 \approx -0.00437
\]

Thus, \(x \approx -0.00437\).
