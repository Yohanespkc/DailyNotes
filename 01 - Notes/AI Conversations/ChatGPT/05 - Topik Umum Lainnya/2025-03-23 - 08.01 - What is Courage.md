### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Courage is the ability to face fear, pain, danger, uncertainty, or even rejection, despite feeling afraid or unsure. It’s not about being fearless—it’s about acting **in spite** of fear.

Courage can take many forms:
- **Physical courage**: risking harm for a cause or to protect others.
- **Moral courage**: standing up for what’s right, even when it’s unpopular or hard.
- **Emotional courage**: being vulnerable, honest, or expressing feelings, even when it's uncomfortable.

In short, courage is what pushes someone to do the right thing or take a necessary risk—even when it’s tough. Want to dive deeper into a certain type of courage?
