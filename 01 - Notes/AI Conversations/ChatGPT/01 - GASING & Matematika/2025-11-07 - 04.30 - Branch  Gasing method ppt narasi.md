# 🎤 **Speech 15 Menit – “The GASING Method: Giving Meaning to Intelligence”**
*(By Prof. Yohanes Surya – GASING Academy, Indonesia)*

---

### **1. Opening – A Greeting from the Islands (2 minutes)**

> Your Eminences, Excellencies, distinguished guests, ladies and gentlemen —  
>  
> I come from Indonesia — a land of more than seventeen thousand islands,  
> a mosaic of cultures, languages, and faiths.  
>  
> In some of these islands, children walk for hours just to go to school.  
> Many study without books, sometimes even without electricity.  
>  
> And yet… when they are given the chance to learn in the right way,  
> something extraordinary happens — their eyes light up,  
> their confidence returns,  
> and their laughter fills the classroom.  
>  
> That is what I call **intelligence with meaning**.  
>  
> Today, I am honored to share with you a small story from Indonesia —  
> the story of **GASING** — a method that makes learning  
> *Gampang, Asyik, dan Menyenangkan* —  
> which means *Easy, Joyful, and Meaningful.*

---

### **2. The Birth of GASING (3 minutes)**

> GASING was born from a question that kept me awake at night:  
> “Why do so many children believe that mathematics is only for the smart?”  
>  
> I was a physicist who studied quarks and quantum particles.  
> But when I returned home, I found a deeper mystery —  
> the fear of numbers.  
>  
> So I went to Papua, one of the most remote provinces of Indonesia.  
> There, I met children who could barely count to ten.  
>  
> We started not with formulas — but with stones, sticks, and games.  
> We sang, we clapped, we played.  
>  
> Within three months, those children could add, subtract, multiply, and divide —  
> and most importantly, they were **smiling**.  
>  
> That is when I realized:  
> the problem was never in the child.  
> The problem was in the way we teach.  
>  
> **Every child can learn — if we teach in the right way.**

---

### **3. The Philosophy of GASING (3 minutes)**

> The GASING method is simple, yet powerful.  
> It follows a natural human journey:  
> **Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract.**  
>  
> First, children touch and move.  
> Then they draw and visualize.  
> Only then do they use symbols and formulas.  
>  
> Learning becomes a joyful discovery, not a memorization.  
>  
> Every step is built so small that no child ever feels stupid.  
> And when they succeed, their confidence grows — one victory at a time.  
>  
> GASING transforms fear into curiosity,  
> and curiosity into mastery.  
>  
> That’s why we call it *Gampang, Asyik, Menyenangkan* —  
> because learning must be easy, joyful, and meaningful.  

---

### **4. The Results and the Human Story (3 minutes)**

> In more than 125 districts across Indonesia,  
> thousands of teachers have now been trained in the GASING method.  
>  
> From Papua to Sumatra, from Kalimantan to Java,  
> we have seen children who once hid from math  
> now raising their hands eagerly to answer questions.  
>  
> One teacher told me,  
> “Before, my students were afraid when I asked, ‘What’s the answer?’  
> Now, they fight for the chance to explain how they got it.”  
>  
> The change is not just academic — it is human.  
>  
> Children begin to believe in themselves.  
> Teachers rediscover their love for teaching.  
>  
> In Manokwari, even the Vice President of Indonesia joined the children,  
> singing and playing math games together.  
> For a moment, the classroom became a place of laughter, not pressure —  
> and that, I believe, is education at its purest form.  

---

### **5. GASING and Artificial Intelligence (3 minutes)**

> This Congress asks a profound question:  
> *“What is intelligence without meaning?”*  
>  
> Artificial Intelligence can compute faster than any human,  
> but it cannot yet feel wonder, joy, or compassion.  
>  
> GASING reminds us that true intelligence is not just about logic —  
> it is about **connection**.  
>  
> It is when the teacher looks into a child’s eyes and says,  
> “You can do it,”  
> and the child begins to believe.  
>  
> Imagine when AI learns from that —  
> not just to calculate, but to **care**.  
>  
> At GASING Academy, we are now building AI tutors  
> that follow the same learning steps as children —  
> from concrete to abstract —  
> from memorizing to understanding.  
>  
> But we also teach our AI to encourage,  
> to say, “Good job!” when a child improves,  
> and to patiently guide when they struggle.  
>  
> Because the goal is not to replace teachers,  
> but to empower them —  
> to let AI handle complexity,  
> so humans can focus on compassion.

---

### **6. Closing – A Call for Meaningful Intelligence (1–2 minutes)**

> My friends,  
> the world does not need more machines that think.  
> The world needs more humans who feel.  
>  
> GASING is not just a method — it is a **movement of compassion through learning.**  
>  
> From a small classroom in Papua,  
> we learned that the most powerful form of intelligence  
> is not artificial — it is **authentic**.  
>  
> As AI becomes more capable,  
> may we also become more human.  
>  
> Let us bring back the heart into intelligence.  
>  
> Let AI compute,  
> and let humans connect.  
>  
> Together, we can build a future  
> where learning is not only smart —  
> but also kind, joyful, and full of meaning.  
>  
> Thank you.  
> *Terima kasih. Grazie.*
