## **5.4.8 Cortisol: The Stress Hormone That Inhibits Learning**

### **What Is Cortisol?**

Cortisol is a steroid hormone produced by the adrenal glands in response to stress. It is a central component of the fight-or-flight response—the system that prepares the body to confront threats.

From an evolutionary perspective, cortisol is highly adaptive, mobilizing energy for survival. However, in modern learning contexts, **chronic cortisol is a primary enemy of learning**.
