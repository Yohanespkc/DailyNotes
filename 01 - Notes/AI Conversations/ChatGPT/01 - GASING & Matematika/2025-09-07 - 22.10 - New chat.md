# 💬 New chat

**Tanggal:** `2025-09-07 22.10`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
Untuk mengerti AI apa saja yang harus dipelajari mahasiswa

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Kalau mahasiswa ingin **mengerti AI secara utuh**, ada beberapa bidang utama yang sebaiknya dipelajari. Saya susun bertahap agar jelas seperti kurikulum:

---

## 1. Dasar-Dasar Matematika & Statistik
AI dibangun di atas fondasi matematika. Minimal mahasiswa perlu menguasai:
- **Aljabar Linear** → vektor, matriks, operasi dasar (inti dari neural network).
- **Kalkulus** → turunan & gradien (digunakan dalam backpropagation).
- **Probabilitas & Statistik** → distribusi, ekspektasi, variansi, Bayesian thinking.
- **Optimisasi** → gradient descent, convex optimization.

---

## 2. Dasar-Dasar Ilmu Komputer
- **Struktur Data & Algoritma** → array, tree, graph, sorting, dynamic programming.
- **Kompleksitas Waktu/Ruang** → penting untuk efisiensi AI.
- **Bahasa Pemrograman** → Python (utama), plus dasar C++/Java untuk performa.
- **Sistem Operasi & Networking** (opsional tapi membantu untuk deployment AI).

---

## 3. Machine Learning (ML)
- **Supervised Learning** → regresi, klasifikasi, decision trees, SVM, ensemble (Random Forest, XGBoost).
- **Unsupervised Learning** → clustering (k-means), PCA, dimensionality reduction.
- **Reinforcement Learning** → agen, reward, Q-learning, policy gradient.
- **Evaluasi Model** → overfitting, cross-validation, confusion matrix.

---

## 4. Deep Learning
- **Neural Networks** → feedforward, backpropagation.
- **CNN (Convolutional Neural Networks)** → computer vision.
- **RNN, LSTM, GRU** → sequential data (teks, suara).
- **Transformer & Attention Mechanism** → inti dari ChatGPT, BERT, dll.
- **Framework** → PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras.

---

## 5. Data Handling & Engineering
- **Big Data** → Hadoop, Spark (konsep dasar).
- **Database** → SQL, NoSQL.
- **Data Preprocessing** → cleaning, feature engineering.
- **MLOps** → CI/CD untuk model, deployment di cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure).

---

## 6. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Tokenisasi, word embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe).
- Transformer-based models (BERT, GPT, LLaMA).
- Aplikasi: chatbot, summarization, translation.

---

## 7. Computer Vision
- Image classification, object detection (YOLO, Faster R-CNN).
- Image generation (GANs, diffusion models).
- Aplikasi: face recognition, medical imaging.

---

## 8. Reinforcement Learning & Multi-Agent Systems
- RL klasik (Q-learning).
- Deep RL (DQN, PPO).
- Multi-agent cooperation/competition (digunakan di robotika & game AI).

---

## 9. Etika & Regulasi AI
- **Bias & Fairness** → bagaimana AI bisa diskriminatif.
- **Explainability** → SHAP, LIME.
- **AI Governance** → regulasi, keamanan, dampak sosial.

---

## 10. Proyek & Praktik
Mahasiswa harus sering latihan:
- Hackathon AI.
- Mini-proyek: prediksi harga saham, image classifier, chatbot sederhana.
- Open-source contribution (misalnya HuggingFace, PyTorch repo).

---

👉 Jadi urutannya: **Matematika → Ilmu Komputer → Machine Learning → Deep Learning → Bidang Spesialisasi (NLP, Vision, RL) → Etika & Proyek**.
