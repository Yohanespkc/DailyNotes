## **Closing Speech**  
**3rd Bali Workshop on Music & Brain**

*(Delivered warmly and calmly, with natural pauses)*

Distinguished guests,  
researchers, artists, educators,  
and dear friends from around the world,

Without us realizing it,  
the days we have spent together here in Bali  
have brought us to the closing of this workshop.

Over the past few days,  
we have not only shared knowledge,  
but also shared **experiences, emotions, and human connection**.

And this, in many ways, reflects exactly  
what this workshop has been about:  
music, the brain, and our shared humanity.

*(pause)*

---

Allow me to invite us all, for a moment,  
to step back and look at the bigger picture.

In recent years,  
scientists in China have conducted a series of experiments  
that help us understand the universe  
in a broader and deeper way.

Not by rejecting Einstein—  
because his theories remain a cornerstone of modern science—  
but by **complementing** them.

If we once imagined the universe  
as a vast machine governed only by position and time,  
these experiments suggest another image:

At its most fundamental level,  
the universe also behaves like **an orchestra**.

Not only particles in motion,  
but **waves in resonance**,  
phases adjusting to one another,  
and systems functioning through **synchronization**.

In simple terms,  
reality is not only about *where things are*,  
but about *how they are aligned with one another*.

*(pause)*

---

And here, we find a beautiful connection to music.

In music,  
beauty never comes from a single note.  
It emerges from relationships—  
rhythm, harmony, tension, and release.

What science is now revealing about the universe  
is surprisingly close to  
what we feel when we listen to or create music.

This is the natural bridge  
between science, art, and the human brain.

---

This workshop has reminded us  
that music is not just sound,  
and the brain is not just a biological organ.

Music is a **meeting place**—  
between science and art,  
between body and emotion,  
between individuals and communities.

Through presentations, discussions, and performances,  
we have seen how music can:  
reduce stress,  
support mental wellbeing,  
enhance learning and healing,  
and strengthen social connection.

---

What has been especially meaningful  
is the diversity gathered in this room.

We come from different disciplines,  
different cultures,  
and different parts of the world.

Yet when music is shared,  
when rhythm is experienced together,  
those differences do not divide us—  
they **bring us into alignment**.

Synchronization, here,  
has not been just a scientific concept.  
It has been a lived human experience.

*(pause)*

---

In a short while,  
you will all return to your respective places:  
to laboratories,  
classrooms,  
concert halls,  
communities, and families.

Our hope is that you take home  
not only data, notes, or new ideas,  
but also a sense of **connection**  
that was formed here.

That in every future research project,  
artistic creation,  
or technological innovation,  
we remember one simple truth:

Behind every equation and algorithm,  
there is a human being.  
There is emotion.  
There is the rhythm of life.

---

And allow me to close  
with a gentle lesson from Bali.

In Balinese culture,  
life is understood as a continuous effort  
to maintain **balance and harmony**—  
between human beings,  
between humans and nature,  
and between the inner and outer worlds.

Music, dance, and ritual in Bali  
are not merely performances.  
They are ways of living.  
Ways of keeping life in rhythm.

As you return to your countries,  
may you carry with you  
not only new knowledge,  
but also a renewed sense of balance—  
and the inspiration to cultivate harmony  
in your work, your research,  
and your everyday lives.

*(pause, softly)*

---

On behalf of the organizers and hosts,  
thank you for your presence,  
your contributions,  
and your shared journey here.

Safe travels,  
welcome home,  
and may we meet again  
in the same rhythm  
in the future.

Thank you.
