# 💬 Perhitungan segitiga ABC

**Tanggal:** `2025-10-18 18.58`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
7.	Diberikan ∆ ABC dengan b = 10, c = 5, dan   sudut B = 120 derajat.  Tentukan panjang a dan     sudut A,  sudut  C.

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Diketahui  
- \(b=10\) (di depan ∠B)  
- \(c=5\) (di depan ∠C)  
- \(\angle B =120^\circ\)

---

### 1) Mencari sisi \(a\)

Gunakan hukum cosinus pada sudut \(B\):

\[
b^2 = a^2 + c^2 -2ac\cos B
\]

Substitusi:
\[
10^2 = a^2 + 5^2 -2\cdot a \cdot 5 \cdot \cos 120^\circ
\]

\(\cos 120^\circ = -\frac12\)

\[
100 = a^2 +25 -2\cdot 5\cdot a \cdot \left(-\frac12\right)
\]
\[
100 = a^2 +25 +5a
\]
\[
a^2 +5a -75 = 0
\]

Persamaan kuadrat:  
\[
a=\frac{-5 + \sqrt{25+300}}{2} = \frac{-5+ \sqrt{325}}{2} = \frac{-5 + 5\sqrt{13}}{2}
\]

Ambil yang positif:
\[
a = \frac{5(\sqrt{13}-1)}{2} \approx 6{,}514
\]

---

### 2) Mencari sudut \(A\)

Hukum sinus:

\[
\frac{\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{b}
\Rightarrow \sin A = \frac{a\cdot \sin 120^\circ}{10}
\]

\(\sin 120^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\approx0{,}8660\)

\[
\sin A = \frac{6{,}514 \cdot 0{,}8660}{10} \approx 0{,}565
\]
\[
A \approx \arcsin(0{,}565) \approx 34{,}4^\circ
\]

Tidak ada solusi kedua karena \(B=120^\circ\).

---

### 3) Sudut \(C\)

Jumlah sudut segitiga:
\[
C = 180^\circ - 120^\circ -34{,}4^\circ = 25{,}6^\circ
\]

---

## **Jawaban akhir**
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
a &\approx 6{,}514\\
A &\approx 34{,}4^\circ\\
C &\approx 25{,}6^\circ
\end{aligned}
}
\]

### 👤 User:
Diberikan ∆ ABC dengan a = 2, c = 3, dan   sudut A = 30.  Tentukan panjang b dan  sudut  B, sudut  C. Ada 2 kemungkinan jawaban

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Kita punya \(a=2\), \(c=3\), dan \(\angle A=30^\circ\).

Pakai Hukum Sinus:
\[
\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{c}{\sin C}=\frac{b}{\sin B}
\Rightarrow \sin C=\frac{c\sin A}{a}=\frac{3\cdot \tfrac12}{2}=\frac34.
\]

Karena \(\sin C=\tfrac34\), ada **dua** kemungkinan:
1) \(C_1=\arcsin\!\left(\tfrac34\right)\approx 48.590^\circ\)  
2) \(C_2=180^\circ- C_1\approx 131.410^\circ\)

Lalu \(\displaystyle B=180^\circ-A-C\) dan \(\displaystyle b=\frac{a}{\sin A}\sin B=4\sin B\).

### Solusi 1
\[
\begin{aligned}
C &\approx 48.590^\circ,\\
B &=180^\circ-30^\circ-48.590^\circ\approx 101.410^\circ,\\
b &=4\sin(101.410^\circ)\approx 3.921.
\end{aligned}
\]

### Solusi 2
\[
\begin{aligned}
C &\approx 131.410^\circ,\\
B &=180^\circ-30^\circ-131.410^\circ\approx 18.590^\circ,\\
b &=4\sin(18.590^\circ)\approx 1.275.
\end{aligned}
\]

**Ringkas:**
- \((B, C, b)\approx (101.410^\circ,\ 48.590^\circ,\ 3.921)\)
- \((B, C, b)\approx (18.590^\circ,\ 131.410^\circ,\ 1.275)\)
