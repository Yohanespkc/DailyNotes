# 💬 Hitung tan 2x 2y

**Tanggal:** `2025-10-19 19.32`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
	Jika tan⁡〖(x+y)=m〗  dan tan⁡〖(x-y)=n〗,  hitunglah 
	tan⁡2x
	tan⁡2y

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Diketahui  
\[
\tan(x+y)=m \quad\text{dan}\quad \tan(x-y)=n
\]

---

### 1) Mencari \(\tan 2x\)

Gunakan identitas
\[
2x = (x+y)+(x-y)
\]
maka
\[
\tan 2x=\tan\big((x+y)+(x-y)\big)
=\frac{\tan(x+y)+\tan(x-y)}{1-\tan(x+y)\tan(x-y)}
\]

Substitusi \(m\) dan \(n\):
\[
\tan 2x=\frac{m+n}{1-mn}
\]

---

### 2) Mencari \(\tan 2y\)

Gunakan identitas
\[
2y = (x+y) - (x-y)
\]
maka
\[
\tan 2y=\tan\big((x+y)-(x-y)\big)
= \frac{\tan(x+y)-\tan(x-y)}{1+\tan(x+y)\tan(x-y)}
\]

Substitusi \(m\) dan \(n\):
\[
\tan 2y = \frac{m-n}{1+mn}
\]

---

## Jawaban Akhir

\[
\boxed{\tan 2x = \frac{m+n}{\,1-mn\,}}
\qquad,\qquad
\boxed{\tan 2y = \frac{m-n}{\,1+mn\,}}
\]
