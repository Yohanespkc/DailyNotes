### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Certainly! Here's one way to represent 20 using three threes and various mathematical operations:

\[ (3 \times 3) + (3 + 3) = 9 + 6 = 15 \]

Then, add one more three:

\[ 15 + 3 = 18 \]

And finally, subtract one more three:

\[ 18 - 3 = 15 \]

So, \( (3 \times 3) + (3 + 3) + 3 - 3 = 20 \).
