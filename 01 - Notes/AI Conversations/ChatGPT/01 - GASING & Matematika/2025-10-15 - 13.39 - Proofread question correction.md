# 💬 Proofread question correction

**Tanggal:** `2025-10-15 13.39`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
proofread: why GASING is so succesful?

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
The correct version is:  

✅ **Why is GASING so successful?**  

Explanation:  
- In English, when forming a question, the auxiliary verb **“is”** must come before the subject (“GASING”).  
- Also, **“successful”** has a double “s” and ends with **“-ful”**, not “succesful.”
