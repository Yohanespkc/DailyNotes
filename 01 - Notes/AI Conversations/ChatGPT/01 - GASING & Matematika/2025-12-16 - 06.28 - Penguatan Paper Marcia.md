## GASING as an Integrated Pedagogical Framework: Beyond Numeracy Improvement

The findings presented in this study demonstrate that the GASING method is not merely an instructional technique for improving arithmetic speed, but rather a **comprehensive pedagogical framework** that integrates cognitive, emotional, social, and cultural dimensions of learning. The substantial gains observed in students’ mathematical performance—often exceeding 100–200% and, in some contexts, reaching 800–1000%—cannot be adequately explained by drill-based practice alone. Instead, these outcomes reflect the synergistic interaction of multiple educational principles embedded within the GASING approach fileciteturn0file0fileciteturn0file4.

At its core, GASING operationalizes a **mastery-based learning trajectory**, structured around clearly defined “critical points” (titik kritis) at each mathematical level. Once students reach these critical points—such as mastery of addition results below 20—they exhibit a qualitative shift in learning behavior, allowing subsequent concepts to be acquired with markedly reduced cognitive load. This phenomenon aligns with mastery learning theory and cognitive load theory, which emphasize the importance of automatising foundational schemas before introducing higher-level abstractions.

### Integration of Major Educational Theories

One of the key contributions of the GASING method lies in its ability to **synthesize multiple educational theories into a coherent classroom practice**, rather than adopting them in isolation fileciteturn0file1. Elements of **behaviorism** are evident in the use of frequent practice, rapid feedback, and positive reinforcement through praise and games. These mechanisms help establish confidence and reduce avoidance behaviors commonly associated with mathematics anxiety.

Simultaneously, GASING is deeply grounded in **cognitivist principles**, particularly through its concrete–abstract–mental calculation (mencongak) progression. Students are first introduced to mathematical concepts using tangible objects and visual representations, before transitioning to symbolic abstraction and internalized mental computation. This progression resonates strongly with Bruner’s enactive–iconic–symbolic representation framework and Ausubel’s emphasis on meaningful learning through structured cognitive scaffolding.

From a **constructivist and sociocultural perspective**, GASING classrooms function as interactive learning communities. Students regularly explain reasoning to peers, collaborate in games, and learn through social modeling. Teachers act not merely as transmitters of knowledge, but as facilitators who guide learners through their Zone of Proximal Development until independence is achieved. This social dimension is particularly salient in teacher-training contexts, where the transformation of teacher mindset becomes a multiplier effect for long-term educational change.

Equally important is the **humanistic foundation** of GASING, often referred to as its “pedagogy of love” or compassion-based teaching. In GASING classrooms, errors are treated as natural steps in learning rather than failures. This emotionally safe environment fosters intrinsic motivation, self-efficacy, and resilience—factors consistently shown to predict long-term academic engagement.

### GASING, 8C Competencies, and Human Capacity Building

Beyond improving numeracy, GASING systematically cultivates the eight core competencies known as the **8C framework**: Character, Creativity, Critical Thinking, Communication, Collaboration, Computational Logic, Culture, and Compassion fileciteturn0file2. Unlike many 21st-century skills frameworks that remain aspirational, GASING embeds these competencies directly into daily classroom practice.

For instance, computational logic is developed not by memorization of results, but by requiring students to articulate the reasoning process behind each calculation. Compassion is cultivated through structured peer support, where students are encouraged—and expected—to assist classmates who struggle. Cultural competence emerges naturally as teachers and students incorporate local songs, languages, and everyday objects into mathematical explanations, making learning contextually meaningful and socially grounded.

This holistic orientation aligns GASING with global educational priorities, including PISA’s emphasis on problem-solving, reasoning, and adaptability. Importantly, GASING demonstrates that such competencies can be effectively developed **through foundational mathematics instruction**, rather than through separate or additional curricular interventions.

## Why Joyful, Multisensory Learning Works

The effectiveness of the GASING method can also be understood through the lens of **educational neuroscience**, which provides a biological explanation for the rapid and sustained learning gains observed in diverse contexts, including under-resourced and remote regions fileciteturn0file0fileciteturn0file1.

Contemporary neuroscience research indicates that learning is optimized when cognitive processes are supported by **positive emotional states**, multisensory input, and meaningful social interaction. GASING intentionally creates such conditions through music, movement, rhythm, laughter, and collective participation. These elements activate multiple neural networks simultaneously, enhancing synaptic connectivity and memory consolidation.

### Emotion, Anxiety Reduction, and Cognitive Performance

Mathematics anxiety has been shown to activate neural circuits associated with fear and even physical pain, thereby consuming working memory resources essential for problem-solving. GASING directly counteracts this mechanism by establishing an emotionally safe and joyful learning environment. When students experience success early and frequently—supported by praise and playful activities—the amygdala’s threat response is reduced, allowing the prefrontal cortex to engage more fully in reasoning and abstraction.

This neuro-emotional regulation helps explain why students who previously avoided mathematics can rapidly transition into confident and enthusiastic learners. The visible indicators frequently reported by trainers—“sparkling eyes,” spontaneous laughter, and eagerness to participate—are consistent with a neurobiological state conducive to learning.

### Multisensory Engagement and Brain Plasticity

GASING’s use of finger movements, body gestures, singing, and visual imagination engages motor, auditory, visual, and cognitive systems concurrently. Neuroscientific studies suggest that such multisensory engagement strengthens hippocampal function and promotes long-term potentiation, a neural mechanism underlying durable learning. The emphasis on mental calculation (mencongak) further trains visual-spatial working memory, as students are required to internally represent and manipulate numerical structures.

This multisystem activation may account for GASING’s observed transfer effects, where improvements in mathematical performance are accompanied by gains in attention, confidence, communication skills, and even leadership behaviors.

### Implications for Equity and Scalability

From a neurocognitive standpoint, GASING’s reliance on low-tech, body-based, and emotionally supportive strategies makes it particularly well-suited for contexts with limited infrastructure. Rather than compensating for material scarcity, GASING leverages **universally available human resources**—movement, voice, rhythm, and social connection—to unlock learning potential. This characteristic helps explain the method’s success across highly diverse socio-economic and cultural settings.

Hasil penelitian ini menunjukkan bahwa metode GASING bukan sekadar teknik untuk meningkatkan kecepatan berhitung, melainkan sebuah **kerangka pedagogis terintegrasi** yang menyatukan aspek kognitif, emosional, sosial, dan kultural dalam proses pembelajaran. Inti dari metode GASING adalah pendekatan **mastery learning** yang menekankan pencapaian “titik kritis” pada setiap jenjang konsep matematika. Ketika siswa telah menguasai titik kritis—misalnya penjumlahan dengan hasil di bawah 20—terjadi perubahan kualitatif dalam cara mereka belajar. Konsep lanjutan dapat dipelajari dengan jauh lebih mudah karena beban kognitif berkurang dan struktur konsep dasar telah terbentuk dengan kuat. Fenomena ini sejalan dengan teori beban kognitif dan pembelajaran berbasis penguasaan, yang menekankan pentingnya otomatisasi konsep fundamental sebelum berpindah ke tingkat yang lebih kompleks.

### Integrasi Teori-Teori Pendidikan Utama

Salah satu kekuatan utama metode GASING terletak pada kemampuannya **mengintegrasikan berbagai aliran teori pendidikan ke dalam praktik kelas yang utuh**, bukan menerapkannya secara terpisah-pisah. Pada saat yang sama, GASING sangat kuat dalam aspek **kognitivisme**, khususnya melalui tahapan pembelajaran konkret–abstrak–mencongak. Siswa tidak langsung dihadapkan pada simbol atau rumus, tetapi terlebih dahulu membangun pemahaman melalui objek nyata dan visualisasi. Setelah itu, mereka diarahkan menuju abstraksi simbolik dan akhirnya pada perhitungan mental. Pola ini selaras dengan kerangka representasi Bruner serta prinsip pembelajaran bermakna yang menekankan struktur kognitif yang jelas.

Dari perspektif **konstruktivisme dan sosiokultural**, pembelajaran GASING berlangsung secara aktif dan kolaboratif. Siswa sering diminta menjelaskan proses berpikir kepada teman, berdiskusi, dan belajar melalui permainan kelompok. Guru berperan sebagai fasilitator yang membimbing siswa dalam Zona Perkembangan Proksimal hingga mereka mencapai kemandirian. Dalam konteks pelatihan guru, pendekatan ini tidak hanya meningkatkan kemampuan mengajar, tetapi juga mentransformasi cara pandang guru terhadap matematika dan potensi siswa.

Landasan **humanistik** juga sangat menonjol dalam GASING, yang sering disebut sebagai pedagogi berbasis kasih atau *pedagogy of love*. Kesalahan dipandang sebagai bagian alami dari proses belajar, bukan sebagai kegagalan. Lingkungan belajar yang aman secara emosional ini menumbuhkan motivasi intrinsik, keberanian mencoba, serta ketahanan belajar (resilience), yang merupakan faktor kunci keberhasilan jangka panjang.
