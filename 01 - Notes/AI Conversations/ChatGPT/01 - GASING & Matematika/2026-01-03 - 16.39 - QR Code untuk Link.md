**Self-Determination Theory (SDT)** adalah **teori motivasi psikologis** yang menjelaskan **mengapa manusia mau belajar, bekerja, dan bertumbuh dengan sungguh-sungguh dari dalam dirinya**, bukan karena paksaan atau hadiah semata.

Teori ini dikembangkan oleh **entity["people","Edward L. Deci","psychology researcher"]** dan **entity["people","Richard M. Ryan","psychology researcher"]**.

---

## 🌱 Inti Utama Self-Determination Theory

SDT menyatakan bahwa **manusia secara alami termotivasi** jika **tiga kebutuhan psikologis dasar** berikut terpenuhi:

### 1️⃣ **Autonomy (Otonomi)**  
> *Saya merasa memilih dan memiliki kendali atas apa yang saya lakukan.*

- Belajar karena **ingin**, bukan karena dipaksa
- Guru memberi ruang eksplorasi, bukan hanya instruksi kaku

### 2️⃣ **Competence (Kompetensi)**  
> *Saya merasa mampu dan berkembang.*

- Tantangan **pas di level kemampuan**
- Ada progres yang terasa (dari bisa sedikit → bisa banyak)

### 3️⃣ **Relatedness (Keterhubungan)**  
> *Saya merasa diterima, dihargai, dan tidak sendirian.*

- Hubungan hangat guru–murid
- Lingkungan aman secara emosional

---

## 🎯 Jenis Motivasi dalam SDT

SDT membedakan motivasi menjadi:

### 🔹 **Motivasi Intrinsik**
Belajar karena **senang & bermakna**
> “Saya belajar matematika karena asyik.”

### 🔹 **Motivasi Ekstrinsik**
Belajar karena **dorongan luar**
> nilai, hadiah, hukuman  
(dalam SDT, ini bisa *diinternalisasi* menjadi motivasi sehat)

### 🔹 **Amotivasi**
Tidak ada dorongan
> “Saya tidak tahu kenapa harus belajar ini.”

---

## 🧠 Mengapa SDT sangat penting untuk pendidikan?

Riset menunjukkan bahwa ketika **autonomy–competence–relatedness terpenuhi**:
- 📈 Motivasi belajar meningkat
- 😄 Emosi positif meningkat
- 🧠 Pemahaman lebih dalam
- 🕒 Retensi belajar lebih lama
- 😌 Kecemasan belajar menurun

## **The Five-Layer GASING Pedagogical Architecture**

GASING is built on a structured convergence of educational theories:

1. **Classical Learning Theories**  
   (Behaviorism, Cognitivism, Constructivism)  
   → *How does learning happen?*

2. **Humanistic & Sociocultural Theories**  
   (Maslow, Rogers, Vygotsky, Dweck)  
   → *Under what conditions does learning work best?*

3. **Educational Neuroscience**  
   (Neuroplasticity, Neurochemistry)  
   → *Why does GASING work in the brain?*

4. **Experiential Learning & Compassion-Based Pedagogy**  
   → *What is the core learning experience in GASING?*

5. **Transformation of Multiple Intelligences into 8C**  
   → *What outcomes does GASING produce?*

---

## **Unlocking the Full Spectrum of Intelligence**

Every child is born with a **complete spectrum of intelligence potential**—a constellation waiting to be activated.

Howard Gardner’s **Multiple Intelligences Theory** showed the world that intelligence is **not a single IQ score**, but a mosaic of abilities, including:

- Linguistic  
- Logical–Mathematical  
- Visual–Spatial  
- Musical  
- Bodily–Kinesthetic  
- Interpersonal  
- Intrapersonal  
- Naturalistic  

**The key point is this:**  
Every child has *all* of these intelligences.

No intelligence is naturally weak or dominant.  
Dominance appears **only when focus and opportunity are given**.

For example, an athlete does not rely only on physical intelligence.  
They also need:
- logic (strategy),
- interpersonal intelligence (teamwork),
- intrapersonal intelligence (mental resilience).

---

## **The Tragedy of Buried Potential**

Prof. Yohanes Surya’s story illustrates how potential can be buried by a single sentence.

As a high school student, his choir teacher once said:  
> *“Yohanes, your singing is always off-key.”*

From that moment, he closed himself off from music and focused entirely on mathematics and physics. This path led him to become a leading physicist and to guide Indonesia to international science Olympiad gold medals.

Years later, a talent assessment revealed something surprising:  
**he also had strong potential as a music composer.**

This is evident today—almost all GASING songs are written by him.

How many children hear sentences like:
- *“I’m not good at math,”*
- *“I’m not talented,”*

and unknowingly close the door to entire worlds of logic, creativity, and problem-solving?

These **limiting beliefs** silently block human potential.

**The good news:**  
Anyone can develop all intelligences if given the right focus and pathway.  
This is the essence of **Layer 5 of the GASING system**.

---

## **Learning Modalities: The Bridge to Intelligence**

If intelligence is **potential**, then **modality is the pathway**.

A **learning modality** is:
> *The primary way the brain receives, processes, and expresses information.*

In simple terms:
> **Modality = how the brain works**

Modalities are **not just “learning styles.”**  
They are real **neurobiological and sensorimotor pathways**.

Common modalities include:
- Visual (images, diagrams, colors)
- Auditory (sound, rhythm, songs)
- Kinesthetic (movement, hands-on tools)
- Verbal (language, stories)
- Social (interaction, collaboration)
- Emotional (safety, joy, confidence)

---

## **Intelligence vs. Modality (Key Difference)**

- **Intelligence** = *what potential exists inside a child*  
- **Modality** = *how that potential is accessed most effectively*

One intelligence can be activated through **multiple modalities**.

For example, logical–mathematical intelligence in GASING is developed through:
- hands-on tools (kinesthetic),
- diagrams (visual),
- stories (linguistic),
- group discussion (social).

This is why GASING is called **Multimodal Learning**—  
it opens **many doors to the brain**, not just one.

---

## **From Intelligence to 8C: The Outcome**

When intelligence is developed through the right modalities, the result is **21st-century competence**, formulated in GASING as **8C**:

- **Collaboration**  
- **Critical Thinking**  
- **Creativity**  
- **Communication**  
- **Computational Logic**  
- **Character**  
- **Culture**  
- **Compassion**

These are not merely technical skills.  
They integrate **cognitive, social, emotional, and moral dimensions**.

This is what distinguishes GASING graduates:  
they are not only smart—but also resilient, empathetic, and grounded.

---

## **The GASING Way: KRILANGKUN Framework**

The transformation process follows a simple but powerful structure:

- **KRI (Critical Target):**  
  Develop all intelligences

- **LANG (Steps):**  
  Use the right learning modalities

- **KUN (Perseverance):**  
  Apply them consistently and wholeheartedly

When these three align, **Mestakung** happens—  
*the universe supports the process*.

The outcome—**8C competence**—is a natural result, not an accident.
