# GASING: An Easy, Fun, and Enjoyable Method for Learning Mathematics

## Abstract

The GASING method (an acronym for *Easy, Fun, and Enjoyable*) was developed by Prof. Yohanes Surya as an innovative approach to address low numeracy levels in Indonesia, particularly in underdeveloped regions. This method emphasizes a gradual learning process from **concrete → abstract → mental calculation**, combined with an active and enjoyable classroom atmosphere, to reduce math anxiety and foster students’ self-confidence.

This paper reviews the core philosophy of GASING, its implementation strategies, and its empirical impact recorded across more than **121 districts and municipalities** in Indonesia, including remote and underserved (3T) areas. Evaluation results show a very significant increase in post-test mathematics scores, averaging **100–200% higher** than pre-test scores, with some cases achieving **over 800–1000% improvement**. A marked increase in students’ interest and positive attitudes toward mathematics was also observed.

Qualitative impacts include a transformation in the mindset of both teachers and students, strengthening character and **21st-century skills (8Cs)** while supporting the development of **multiple intelligences**. This success opens opportunities for national and international replication, in line with the *Indonesia Emas 2045* vision, which emphasizes the strengthening of numeracy as a foundation for scientific and technological literacy.

---

# 1. Problem Background

Basic mathematics is a crucial foundation for building high-quality human resources. Early mastery of mathematics supports understanding in other subjects, strengthens logical thinking, problem-solving skills, and adaptability. Strengthening basic mathematics is therefore a key strategy for realizing the vision of **Indonesia Emas 2045**.

However, mathematics is still widely perceived as difficult and intimidating. Results from **PISA 2022** show that Indonesian students achieved an average mathematics score of **366**, far below the OECD average of **438**, placing Indonesia **69th out of 80 countries** (OECD, 2023). Disparities are especially severe in **3T regions**, where shortages of trained teachers and limited resources persist.

Neuroscience research shows that math anxiety activates brain regions associated with physical pain (Lyons & Beilock, 2012). Traditional drill-based instruction often fails to develop **critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, and communication**, which are essential 21st-century competencies.

---

# 2. Why GASING?

Mathematics instruction in Indonesia has long relied on rote memorization and mechanical procedures. Such approaches contribute to shallow understanding and high math anxiety (Foley et al., 2017).

The **GASING method**, developed over two decades ago, offers an alternative by emphasizing **deep conceptual understanding**, logical structure, and joyful learning. Mathematics is presented as an interconnected system rather than isolated formulas.

> *“There is no such thing as a stupid child; there are only children who have not yet been given the opportunity to learn with good teachers and good methods.”*  
> — Yohanes Surya

GASING begins with concrete representations, transitions to abstract symbols, and culminates in **mental arithmetic**, all within a playful and engaging environment involving games, songs, and movement.

---

# 3. GASING Method: Philosophy and Core Components

## 3.1 Core Philosophy

GASING is founded on the belief that **all children can learn mathematics** when fear is removed and appropriate pedagogy is used. Learning difficulties arise not from lack of intelligence, but from anxiety and misconceptions about mathematics.

The method emphasizes:
- Mastery learning with **critical points**
- Pattern recognition and structure
- A shift in mindset from fear to confidence

## 3.2 Concrete–Abstract–Mental Arithmetic Progression

1. **Concrete Stage**  
   Concepts are introduced using real, tangible objects.

2. **Abstract Stage**  
   Mathematical symbols are introduced as representations of known concepts.

3. **Mental Arithmetic Stage**  
   Students perform rapid calculations mentally, strengthening cognitive flexibility and brain coordination.

## 3.3 Joyful Learning Environment

Learning activities include:
- Songs for memorization
- Games for speed and accuracy
- Movement and rhythm for engagement

Through intensive practice, students can master the entire elementary mathematics curriculum in **approximately six months**.

---

# 4. Development of the 8Cs and Multiple Intelligences

## 4.1 The 8Cs Framework

GASING integrates **21st-century skills (8Cs)** organically:

- **Character**
- **Critical Thinking**
- **Creativity**
- **Communication**
- **Collaboration**
- **Computational Logic**
- **Compassion**
- **Culture**

These competencies emerge naturally through discussion, peer teaching, cultural integration, and collaborative problem-solving.

## 4.2 Multiple Intelligences

Aligned with Gardner’s theory, GASING nurtures:
- Logical-mathematical
- Visual-spatial
- Musical
- Kinesthetic
- Linguistic
- Interpersonal
- Intrapersonal
- Naturalistic intelligences

GASING thus supports holistic human development rather than narrow academic performance.

---

# 5. Implementation of the GASING Method in Indonesia

Since 2022, GASING has been implemented in over **110 districts**, reaching:
- **11,000+ teachers**
- **13,700+ students**

Earlier programs involved representatives from **250 districts** nationwide. Implementation is supported by local governments, NGOs, and CSR partnerships.

---

# 6. Teacher Training and Dissemination Scheme

The standard model includes:
- **Two-week intensive training**
- Certification of local teachers
- A **training-of-trainers (ToT)** system for scalability

Examples include South Tapanuli, Mimika (Papua), and Central Halmahera, where large-scale replication targets tens of thousands of students.

---

# 7. Coverage in 3T Regions

GASING has demonstrated exceptional impact in 3T regions such as:
- Papua (Tolikara, Wamena, Yahukimo)
- Humbang Hasundutan
- South Manokwari
- Central Halmahera

In many cases, students mastered multi-digit arithmetic within two weeks, with **zero dropout** from mastery targets.

---

# 8. Adoption in Urban Areas

Urban adoption includes:
- **Makassar City**, targeting **235,000 students**
- City-wide integration into elementary and junior high schools

This demonstrates GASING’s adaptability across socio-economic contexts.

# 9. Training Outcomes

## 9.1 Quantitative Results

- Average score increases: **33+ points**
- Pre-test to post-test improvements: **100–1000%**
- Statistically significant gains (*p* < 0.05)

## 9.2 Qualitative Outcomes

Testimonials reveal:
- Reduced math anxiety

# 10. Vision and Future of GASING

Long-term goals include:

GASING reframes mathematics as **accessible to all**, even in the most remote regions.

# 11. Conclusion

The GASING method has proven effective in improving numeracy, mindset, and character within a short timeframe. Its success across diverse regions suggests strong potential as a **national best practice** in mathematics education.

Sustained collaboration, policy support, and longitudinal research are recommended to ensure long-term impact.

# GASING: Metode Gampang, Asyik, dan Menyenangkan untuk Belajar Matematika

## Abstrak

Metode **GASING** (singkatan dari *Gampang, Asyik, dan Menyenangkan*) dikembangkan oleh Prof. Yohanes Surya sebagai pendekatan inovatif untuk mengatasi rendahnya kemampuan numerasi di Indonesia, khususnya di daerah tertinggal. Metode ini menekankan pembelajaran bertahap dari **konkret–abstrak–mencongak**, serta suasana belajar yang aktif dan menyenangkan, guna mengurangi kecemasan belajar (*math anxiety*) dan menumbuhkan rasa percaya diri siswa.

Paper ini mengulas filosofi dasar GASING, strategi implementasinya, serta dampak empiris yang tercatat di lebih dari **121 kabupaten/kota** di Indonesia, termasuk wilayah **3T**. Hasil evaluasi menunjukkan peningkatan sangat signifikan pada skor *post-test* matematika, rata-rata **100–200%** lebih tinggi dari skor *pre-test*, bahkan ada yang mencapai **800–1000%** peningkatan. Terlihat pula lonjakan minat dan sikap positif siswa terhadap matematika.

Dampak kualitatif tampak pada transformasi *mindset* guru dan siswa, penguatan karakter dan keterampilan abad ke-21 (**8C**), serta dukungan terhadap pengembangan **kecerdasan majemuk**. Keberhasilan ini membuka peluang replikasi nasional dan internasional, sejalan dengan visi **Indonesia Emas 2045**.

## 1. Latar Belakang Masalah

Matematika dasar merupakan fondasi penting dalam membangun sumber daya manusia (SDM) yang unggul. Penguasaan matematika sejak dini tidak hanya membantu memahami mata pelajaran lain, tetapi juga memperkuat keterampilan berpikir logis, pemecahan masalah, dan kemampuan adaptasi. Oleh karena itu, penguatan matematika dasar menjadi strategi kunci untuk mewujudkan visi **Indonesia Emas 2045**.

Namun, matematika masih dianggap sebagai pelajaran yang sulit dan menakutkan. Hasil **PISA 2022** menunjukkan skor rata-rata matematika siswa Indonesia sebesar **366**, jauh di bawah rata-rata OECD **438**, menempatkan Indonesia pada peringkat **69 dari 80 negara** (OECD, 2023). Kesenjangan semakin terasa di wilayah **3T**, yang menghadapi keterbatasan guru terlatih, sumber belajar, dan infrastruktur pendidikan.

Penelitian neurosains menunjukkan bahwa kecemasan terhadap matematika dapat mengaktifkan area otak yang terkait dengan rasa sakit fisik (Lyons & Beilock, 2012). Instruksi berbasis *drill* tradisional juga sering gagal mengembangkan keterampilan berpikir tingkat tinggi seperti **critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, dan communication** (Partnership for 21st Century Skills, 2009).

---

## 2. Mengapa GASING?

Pembelajaran matematika di Indonesia umumnya masih menekankan hafalan rumus dan prosedur mekanistik. Pendekatan ini kerap gagal membangun pemahaman konsep mendalam dan berkontribusi pada tingginya *math anxiety* (Foley et al., 2017).

Metode **GASING**, yang dikembangkan Prof. Yohanes Surya lebih dari dua dekade lalu, hadir sebagai alternatif inovatif dengan fokus pada pemahaman konseptual menyeluruh. Matematika dipandang sebagai **sistem utuh** yang saling terhubung, bukan sekadar kumpulan topik terpisah.

> *“Tidak ada anak yang bodoh; yang ada adalah anak yang belum mendapat kesempatan belajar dengan guru dan metode yang baik.”*  
> — Yohanes Surya

Pembelajaran dimulai dari objek konkret, berlanjut ke simbol abstrak, dan diakhiri dengan **mencongak**. Aktivitas kreatif seperti permainan, bernyanyi, dan gerak tubuh menciptakan suasana belajar yang menyenangkan dan efektif.

---

## 3. Metode GASING: Filosofi dan Komponen Utama

### 3.1 Filosofi Dasar GASING

Metode GASING berangkat dari keyakinan bahwa semua anak dapat belajar matematika dengan metode yang tepat. Kesulitan belajar umumnya bersumber dari rasa takut dan *mindset* keliru, bukan dari keterbatasan kognitif.

Matematika dipahami sebagai sistem terstruktur yang memiliki **titik-titik kritis**. Jika titik ini terlampaui, siswa tidak lagi mengalami kesulitan berarti. Pendekatan *mastery learning* memastikan tidak ada konsep yang terlewat.

### 3.2 Strategi Pembelajaran: Konkret–Abstrak–Mencongak

1. **Konkret**  
   Konsep diperkenalkan melalui objek nyata yang dapat dilihat dan diraba.

2. **Abstrak**  
   Simbol dan notasi matematika dikenalkan sebagai representasi konsep konkret.

3. **Mencongak**  
   Siswa dilatih menghitung cepat secara mental, meningkatkan kecepatan, ketelitian, dan koordinasi otak.

### 3.3 Suasana Belajar Menyenangkan

Pembelajaran diwarnai lagu, permainan, dan aktivitas fisik. Dengan pendekatan ini, siswa dapat menguasai seluruh materi matematika SD yang biasanya ditempuh 6 tahun hanya dalam **±6 bulan intensif**.

---

## 4. Pengembangan 8C dan Kecerdasan Majemuk

### 4.1 Kerangka 8C

GASING mengembangkan keterampilan abad ke-21 (**8C**):

- Communication  
- Collaboration  
- Computational Logic  
- Compassion  
- Culture  

Semua unsur terintegrasi alami dalam aktivitas belajar.

### 4.2 Kecerdasan Majemuk

Sejalan dengan teori Gardner, GASING mengembangkan kecerdasan:
- logika-matematik,
- visual-spasial,
- musikal,
- kinestetik,
- linguistik,
- interpersonal,
- intrapersonal,
- naturalistik.

GASING mendorong perkembangan holistik siswa, bukan sekadar kemampuan berhitung.

---

- **11.000+ guru**
- **13.700+ siswa**

## 6. Cakupan Wilayah 3T dan Perkotaan

GASING diterapkan luas di wilayah 3T seperti Papua, Humbang Hasundutan, Halmahera Tengah, dan Manokwari Selatan, serta mulai diadopsi di kota besar seperti **Makassar** (235.000 siswa).

---

## 8. Visi dan Masa Depan GASING

GASING berpotensi menjadi **model nasional numerasi**, mendukung Indonesia Emas 2045. Pengembangan platform digital memungkinkan jangkauan nasional bahkan internasional.
