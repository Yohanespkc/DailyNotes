Siap! Kita ubah persamaan ke bentuk aljabar sederhana (pakai identitas dasar), lalu selesaikan untuk \(0^\circ \le x < 360^\circ\).

### 8)
**a.** \(8\sin x\cos x-2=0 \;\Rightarrow\; 4\sin2x-2=0 \Rightarrow \sin2x=\tfrac12\)  
\(\Rightarrow 2x=30^\circ,150^\circ,390^\circ,510^\circ\)  
\(\boxed{x=15^\circ,75^\circ,195^\circ,255^\circ}\)

**b.** \(\cos2x+\sqrt2\cos x-1=0 \Rightarrow 2\cos^2x+\sqrt2\cos x-2=0\)  
\(\cos x=\dfrac{-\sqrt2\pm 3\sqrt2}{4}\Rightarrow \cos x=\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\) (yang \(-\sqrt2\) ditolak)  
\(\boxed{x=45^\circ,315^\circ}\)

### 9)
**a.** \(4\sin x\cos x+\sqrt2=0 \Rightarrow 2\sin2x=-\sqrt2 \Rightarrow \sin2x=-\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\)  
\(\boxed{x=112.5^\circ,157.5^\circ,292.5^\circ,337.5^\circ}\)

**b.** \(\cos2x-\sqrt3\cos x+1=0 \Rightarrow 2\cos^2x-\sqrt3\cos x=0\)  
\(\cos x=0\) atau \(\cos x=\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)  
\(\boxed{x=90^\circ,270^\circ,30^\circ,330^\circ}\)

### 10)
**a.** \(2\cos2x=8\cos x+5 \Rightarrow 4\cos^2x-8\cos x-7=0\)  
\(\cos x=\dfrac{2-\sqrt{11}}{2}\) (akar satunya \(>1\), ditolak)  
\(\boxed{x=\arccos\!\big(\tfrac{2-\sqrt{11}}{2}\big),\;360^\circ-\arccos\!\big(\tfrac{2-\sqrt{11}}{2}\big)}\)

**b.** \(\cos2x+\sqrt3\sin x-1=0 \Rightarrow -2\sin^2x+\sqrt3\sin x=0\)  
\(\sin x=0\) atau \(\sin x=\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)  
\(\boxed{x=0^\circ,180^\circ,60^\circ,120^\circ}\)

### 11)
**a.** \(3\tan2x+4\tan x=0\Rightarrow 3\frac{2t}{1-t^2}+4t=0\Rightarrow t(10-4t^2)=0\) dengan \(t=\tan x\)  
\(\tan x=0\) atau \(\tan x=\pm\sqrt{\tfrac52}\)  
\(\boxed{x=0^\circ,180^\circ,\;\arctan\!\sqrt{\tfrac52},\;180^\circ+\arctan\!\sqrt{\tfrac52},\;180^\circ-\arctan\!\sqrt{\tfrac52},\;360^\circ-\arctan\!\sqrt{\tfrac52}}\)

**b.** \(\cos2x-3\cos x+2=0 \Rightarrow 2\cos^2x-3\cos x+1=0\Rightarrow \cos x=1 \text{ atau } \tfrac12\)  
\(\boxed{x=0^\circ,360^\circ,60^\circ,300^\circ}\)

### 12)

**b.** \(2\sin2x+4\cos x=0\Rightarrow 4\cos x(\sin x+1)=0\)  
\(\boxed{x=90^\circ,270^\circ}\)

### 13)
**a.** \(4\sin2x=\tan x\Rightarrow 8\sin x\cos^2x=\sin x\Rightarrow \sin x=0\) atau \(\cos^2x=\tfrac18\)  
\(\boxed{x=0^\circ,180^\circ,\;\arccos\!\tfrac{\sqrt2}{4},\;360^\circ-\arccos\!\tfrac{\sqrt2}{4},\;\arccos\!\big(-\tfrac{\sqrt2}{4}\big),\;360^\circ-\arccos\!\big(-\tfrac{\sqrt2}{4}\big)}\)

**b.** \(2\cos2x+13\sin x=5\Rightarrow 4\sin^2x-13\sin x+3=0\)  
\(\sin x=\dfrac{13\pm11}{8}\Rightarrow \sin x=\tfrac14\) (yang \(3\) ditolak)  
\(\boxed{x=\arcsin\!\tfrac14,\;180^\circ-\arcsin\!\tfrac14}\)

### 14)

\(\boxed{x=45^\circ,135^\circ,225^\circ,315^\circ}\)

**b.** \(4\tan2x\tan x=1 \Rightarrow 8t^2=1-t^2 \Rightarrow t=\tan x=\pm \tfrac13\)  
\(\boxed{x=\arctan\!\tfrac13,\;180^\circ+\arctan\!\tfrac13,\;180^\circ-\arctan\!\tfrac13,\;360^\circ-\arctan\!\tfrac13}\)
