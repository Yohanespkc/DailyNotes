## **3. The Core Product: 5 Main Engines**

To truly reach billions of people, this platform should be built around **five major engines**.

### **A. Assessment Engine**
When a child enters, the AI does not teach immediately.  
First, it carefully maps the child’s current ability.

For example:
- “I want to learn addition.”  
- The AI asks the child’s grade level  
- Then gives a multimodal assessment:
  - concrete: picture of 2 oranges + 3 oranges  
  - abstract: 2 + 3 = ?  
  - audio: mental math through voice  
  - fast visual questions with a timer  
  - handwriting answers that AI can read  
  - spoken answers that AI can hear  

From this, the AI can know:
- whether the child understands the concept  
- whether the child only memorized  
- whether the child is slow  
- whether the child is strong in visual learning but weak in mental math  
- whether the child is ready to move up or needs to go back one step  

This is very important, because many learning platforms fail here.  
They give the same material to everyone.

**GASING × GROK gives the right starting point for each learner.**

---

The AI does not teach in a generic way.

- **Fun Learning** → the concept is taught

- **Fun Adventure** → the concept is used in a world adventure  

For example, for making 10:
- catching stars worth 10  
- opening a gate with the right answer  
- building energy to fight monsters  
- finding number combinations to activate tools  
- timed puzzle games

These games do not need to be created from zero every time.

The platform can use **a large library of game templates, funny backgrounds, animations, characters, and story scenarios** that can be automatically matched to the student’s topic and level.

This makes production much more efficient while still keeping the experience exciting.
