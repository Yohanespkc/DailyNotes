# 💬 Hitung akar persamaan kuadrat

**Tanggal:** `2025-08-24 07.40`  
**Sumber:** ChatGPT Export  

---

### 👤 User:
Hitung akar persamaan kuadrat 	1/2 x^2-1/5 x-1/4=0

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Kita selesaikan persamaan kuadrat:  

\[
\frac{1}{2}x^2 - \frac{1}{5}x - \frac{1}{4} = 0
\]

---

### 1. Samakan koefisien (hilangkan pecahan)  
Kalikan semua ruas dengan **20** (KPK dari 2, 5, dan 4):

\[
20 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2}x^2\right) - 20 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{5}x\right) - 20 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{4}\right) = 0
\]

\[
10x^2 - 4x - 5 = 0
\]

---

### 2. Tentukan diskriminan  
Persamaan: \(10x^2 - 4x - 5 = 0\)  
- \(a = 10\)  
- \(b = -4\)  
- \(c = -5\)  

\[
D = b^2 - 4ac = (-4)^2 - 4(10)(-5) = 16 + 200 = 216
\]

---

### 3. Cari akar-akarnya  
\[
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{D}}{2a} = \frac{-(-4) \pm \sqrt{216}}{20}
\]

\[
x = \frac{4 \pm \sqrt{216}}{20}
\]

---

### 4. Sederhanakan akar  
\[
\sqrt{216} = \sqrt{36 \cdot 6} = 6\sqrt{6}
\]

\[
x = \frac{4 \pm 6\sqrt{6}}{20}
\]

\[
x = \frac{2 \pm 3\sqrt{6}}{10}
\]

---

✅ Jadi akar-akarnya adalah:  

\[
x_1 = \frac{2 + 3\sqrt{6}}{10}, \quad x_2 = \frac{2 - 3\sqrt{6}}{10}
\]

---

### 🤖 ChatGPT:
Oke, mari kita selesaikan:  

\[
\sqrt{55 + 30\sqrt{2}}
\]

---

### 1. Coba bentuk \((a + b\sqrt{2})^2\)  
\[
(a + b\sqrt{2})^2 = a^2 + 2ab\sqrt{2} + 2b^2
\]

Kita ingin:  
\[
a^2 + 2b^2 = 55, \quad 2ab = 30 \;\;\Rightarrow\; ab = 15
\]

---

### 2. Cari pasangan \(a, b\)  
Dari \(ab = 15\).  
Misalkan \(a = \frac{15}{b}\).  

Substitusi ke \(a^2 + 2b^2 = 55\):  

\[
\left(\frac{15}{b}\right)^2 + 2b^2 = 55
\]

\[
\frac{225}{b^2} + 2b^2 = 55
\]

Kalikan \(b^2\):  

\[
225 + 2b^4 = 55b^2
\]

\[
2b^4 - 55b^2 + 225 = 0
\]

---

### 3. Substitusi \(y = b^2\)  
\[
2y^2 - 55y + 225 = 0
\]

Diskriminan:  
\[
D = (-55)^2 - 4(2)(225) = 3025 - 1800 = 1225
\]

\[
\sqrt{1225} = 35
\]

\[
y = \frac{55 \pm 35}{4}
\]

\[
y_1 = \frac{90}{4} = 22.5, \quad y_2 = \frac{20}{4} = 5
\]

---

### 4. Ambil \(b^2 = 5\) (lebih sederhana)  
\[
b = \sqrt{5}
\]

Karena \(ab = 15 \Rightarrow a = \frac{15}{\sqrt{5}} = 3\sqrt{5}
\]

---

### 5. Bentuk solusi  
\[
\sqrt{55 + 30\sqrt{2}} = a + b\sqrt{2} = 3\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{5}\sqrt{2}
\]

\[
= 3\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{10}
\]

---

✅ Jadi:  
\[
\sqrt{55 + 30\sqrt{2}} = 3\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{10}
\]
